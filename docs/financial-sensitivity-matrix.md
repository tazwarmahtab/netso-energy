## 3. Engineering Rigor & Capital Preservation

1. **Mid-Life Battery Refresh Reserve:** Modeled an explicit BDT 10.00 Lakh cash outflow at Year 11 for BESS cell module replacements, ensuring 20-year operational continuity without equity calls.
2. **True Outage Displacement Value:** In Bangladesh residential complexes, running a 150 kVA diesel generator costs BDT 35.00–45.00/kWh in fuel and maintenance. Displacing 250 annual outage hours with solar BESS power delivers BDT 3.2+ Lakh/yr in pure operational cash savings to the building society.
