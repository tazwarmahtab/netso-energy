## Slide 2: The Problem (The "Bleed")
### C&I Margins Are Being Eaten by Grid Volatility & Tariff Shock

- **High Cost:** Commercial grid tariff = **BDT 15.62/kWh** (Source: BPDB, 2025).
- **High Volatility:** Load shedding forces reliance on diesel generators (BDT 22+/kWh equivalent).
- **CAPEX Barrier:** Installing a 50kW system requires **BDT 25–30L upfront** — impossible for mid-sized firms without straining OPEX.
- **Result:** 80% of target C&I buildings are "grid-locked," unable to adopt solar due to capital constraints.

## Slide 3: The Solution (The "Arbitrage")
### Zero-Upfront Solar-as-a-Service at 45% Below Grid Cost

**Model:** RESCO (Revenue Share / Energy Sales)  
**Netso Owns & Operates:** We finance, install, and maintain 100% of the asset.  
**Customer Pays:** Only for power consumed at **BDT 8.50/kWh** (fixed, below-grid).  
**Immediate Win:** Customer saves ~45% instantly; Netso captures the spread over BDT 46.8k/kW CAPEX.  
**Lock-in:** 20-year PPAs with step-up clauses.

## Appendix: Risk Mitigation (Backup Slides)

**1. Grid Interconnection Risk:**  
- *Mitigation:* Policy risk hedged by SREDA mandate. We pre-specify 'off-grid+export' hybrid configs to ensure 80% revenue even if grid tie is delayed.  

**2. Off-taker Credit Risk:**  
- *Mitigation:* PPAs include 'Dearness Allowance' (escalation) + **security deposit (3 months)**. We target investment-grade off-takers (public schools, hospitals) with low default risk.  

**3. Tariff Volatility (Export vs. Retail):**  
- *Mitigation:* Model assumes **conservative base of 8.50**. Arc3 analysis shows 85% upside even if tariff drops to 7.50. We lock 20-year contracts.  

**4. Replication Risk:**  
- *Mitigation:* Modular 'Lego-kit' deployment. Standardized 50kW BOM. 4-week install cycle validated by pilot. We have 5 LOIs in legal review.
