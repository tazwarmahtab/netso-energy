## Executive Summary

Netso Energy has de-risked the RESCO (Revenue Share / Energy Sales) model for commercial rooftop solar in Bangladesh through a pilot-validated deployment. The company offers **zero-upfront solar** at BDT 8.50/kWh (45% below grid tariff), capturing a 42–67% IRR depending on debt tenor structure.

**Key Investment Thesis:**
1. **Proven Unit Economics:** Pilot (4.3kW) validates BDT 46.8k/kW CAPEX, BDT 8.50/kWh export tariff, and 2.6-year payback.
2. **Policy Tailwind:** SREDA Net Metering 2025 + IDCOL 80% debt @ 9% creates a structural arbitrage vs. 18%+ local capital costs.
3. **Defensible Moat:** 20-year PPAs with investment-grade off-takers (schools, hospitals) + exclusive IDCOL credit line access.
4. **Scalable Model:** Modular 50kW "Lego-kit" deployment → 35 sites/yr via $1M equity (triggers $4M total capacity via debt leverage).

**Verdict:** Netso is no longer a "startup" — it is a **yield-generating infrastructure asset** with **66.8% Equity IRR** (levered), **1.8-year equity payback**, and a 17-year profit tail. The risk-adjusted return profile is compelling for climate/infra funds seeking 30%+ IRRs.

## Market Overview

### Problem: The "Grid-Lock" Dilemma
- **Commercial Tariff:** BDT 15.62/kWh (BPDB, 2025) — rising 8–10% YoY.
- **CAPEX Barrier:** 50kW system = BDT 25–30L upfront — prohibitive for mid-sized firms.
- **Result:** 80% of target C&I buildings (BDT 40k+/mo bills) are "grid-locked," unable to adopt solar due to capital constraints.

### Opportunity: 2,000MW TAM
- **Total Viable Rooftop Capacity:** 2,000 MW (Bangladesh commercial/industrial).
- **Active Deployment:** ~245–370 MW (<20% penetration).
- **Untapped SAM:** ~1,630–1,755 MW.
- **Beachhead:** Chattogram + Dhaka mid-sized factories, schools, clinics (1.75MW Y1 target).

### Why Now?
1. **Regulatory Shift:** 2025 Net Metering rules explicitly allow OPEX/RESCO models (previously CAPEX-only).
2. **IDCOL Capital:** 80% debt financing @ 9% for 15–20 years (vs. 18%+ commercial rates).
3. **Grid Stress:** Load shedding + tariff hikes push C&I toward solar hedge strategies.

## Company Overview

### Solution: Zero-Upfront RESCO Model
- **Netso Owns & Operates:** Full CAPEX, installation, O&M.
- **Customer Pays:** Only for power consumed at BDT 8.50/kWh (fixed, 45% below grid).
- **Lock-in:** 20-year PPAs with 8% annual step-up clauses.

### Business Model
- **Revenue:** Long-term index-linked PPA contracts (BDT 8.50/kWh base).
- **Cost Structure:** IDCOL debt (80% @ 9%) + O&M (1% CAPEX/yr).
- **Economics:** 66.8% Equity IRR, 1.8-year equity payback, **2.3x DSCR** (Year 1, Revenue/Debt).

### Traction
- **Pilot:** 4.3kW deployed (Chattogram) — BDT 2.2L actual CAPEX, 98% uptime, 16% capacity factor.
- **Pipeline:** 5 signed LOIs (~200kW), 12 active conversations (>500kW).
- **Partners:** Longi (hardware), Growatt (inverters), IDCOL (credit line pre-approved).
