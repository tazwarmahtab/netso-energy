# Topic Page Template

Use this template for broad subject areas that span multiple entities, concepts, and patterns.

Topics are your "table of contents" pages. They give you and your LLM a quick overview of everything the wiki knows about a given area. They also expose coverage gaps so you know what to research next.

```yaml
---
created: YYYY-MM-DD
updated: YYYY-MM-DD
type: wiki-page
page-type: topic
source-count: 0
sources: []
related: []
---
```
