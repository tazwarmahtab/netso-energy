# Performance Page Template

Use this template to track what's working and what isn't across channels, formats, tactics, and campaign types.

Performance pages turn your GTM execution into a feedback loop. Instead of guessing what works, you build a record. Over time, these pages become your team's playbook for what to repeat and what to kill.
