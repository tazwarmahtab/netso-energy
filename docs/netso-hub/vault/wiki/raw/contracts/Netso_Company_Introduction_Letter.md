### WHO WE ARE

Netso Energy Limited is a registered Renewable Energy Service Company (RESCO) committed to helping Bangladeshi factories reduce electricity costs while supporting the nation's green energy transition. We operate under the Build-Own-Operate (BOO) model, which means:

- **We invest** in the solar system installation
- **We own** the equipment throughout the agreement term
- **We operate** and maintain the system
- **You simply pay** for the electricity you use—at a rate lower than the grid
