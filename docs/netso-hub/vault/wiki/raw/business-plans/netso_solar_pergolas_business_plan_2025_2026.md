Netso is positioned not as a utility provider, but as a **lifestyle technology brand**. In the rapidly evolving landscape of 2025 Bangladesh, where energy independence is becoming a luxury requirement, Netso transforms solar from an industrial eyesore into a premium architectural amenity. By weaving the intricate geometry of **Jamdani heritage** with **Apple-inspired minimalism**, we provide "Sophisticated Sustainability." Our mission is to dominate the **Building-Integrated Photovoltaics (BIPV)** niche, leveraging the 2025 regulatory mandates to become the definitive standard for the modern Bengali home and commercial space.

### 3. GTM ROADMAP: THE SCALED ROLLOUT

#### **Phase 1: Launch (Months 1-6) - The Dhaka Elite**
*   **Geographic Focus:** Gulshan, Banani, Baridhara, and Dhanmondi.
*   **Target Segments:** Penthouse owners and "Elite Commercial Cafes" (Hospitality).
*   **Tactics:**
    1.  **Cinematic Conversion:** Deploy the *Urban Sanctuary* and *Social Hub* videos via geo-fenced Instagram and LinkedIn ads targeting HNWIs and business owners.
    2.  **Pilot Showcases:** Establish three flagship installations (one premium residence, one rooftop cafe, one boutique hotel) to serve as live demonstration sites.
    3.  **Architectural Integration:** Launch a "Netso Design Partner" program for the top 20 architectural firms in Dhaka, providing them with CAD blocks of our pergolas.

#### **Phase 2: Scaling (Months 7-18) - Regional Expansion & Utility Synergy**
*   **Geographic Focus:** Chittagong (Premium Residential & Industrial Offices) and Sylhet (Resorts).
*   **Target Segments:** Real estate developers and utility-scale "Energy Sharing" partners.
*   **Tactics:**
    1.  **Virtual Power Plants (VPP):** Explore Peer-to-Peer (P2P) energy trading partnerships, allowing Netso clusters to trade excess power via the smart grid.
    2.  **Developer Tie-ups:** Sign MOUs with premium developers (e.g., Shanta, Navana) to include Netso Pergolas as a standard feature in new luxury "Green Buildings."
    3.  **Energy-as-a-Service (EaaS):** Formalize the OPEX model where commercial clients pay a monthly fee lower than their current utility bill.

### 4. OPERATIONS & PARTNERSHIPS: THE SUPPLY CHAIN WEAVE
To maintain a 40% gross margin while ensuring premium quality, Netso utilizes a **Hybrid Supply Model**.

*   **Technology Core:** Import Tier-1 Bifacial Glass-on-Glass modules and Hybrid Inverters (SMA/Huawei/Victron) to ensure 25-year longevity.
*   **Structural Localism:** Partner with high-grade local steel and aluminum fabricators (e.g., BSRM/KSRM grade steel) to produce our "Slate Grey" frames. This mitigates import duties and reduces the carbon footprint.
*   **Architectural Synergy:** Collaborate with structural engineers to certify wind-load resistance for 250km/h+ (cyclone-grade resilience).
