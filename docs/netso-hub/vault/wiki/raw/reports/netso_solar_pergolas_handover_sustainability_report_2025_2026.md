The core of the Netso value proposition is measurable environmental resilience. Based on the 2025 energy metrics for the Bangladesh Delta, our installations provide significant carbon offset and clean energy generation.

| **Avg. Daily Solar Yield** | 4.2 kWh/kWp/day | 4.4 kWh/kWp/day |
| **Annual MWh Generation** | **7.66 MWh** | **8.03 MWh** |
| **CO2 Reduction (Annual)** | **5.13 Tons CO2e** | **5.38 Tons CO2e** |
| **25-Year Lifetime Impact** | **191.5 MWh** | **200.7 MWh** |

#### **2.2 Scaled Impact Projection (Year 1 Target: 50 Units)**
By achieving our Year 1 goal of 50 flagship installations across the two primary urban hubs, Netso will contribute the following to the National Renewable Energy Target:

- **Total Clean Energy Generated:** ~385 - 400 MWh/year.
- **Total CO2 Displacement:** ~260 Tons CO2e/year (Equivalent to planting 12,000+ trees).
- **Fossil Fuel Savings:** Offset of approx. 110,000 Liters of furnace oil used in peak-shaving plants.
