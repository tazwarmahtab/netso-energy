
Netso Energy – Building Screening and Proposal Checklist
=======================================================

A. Initial Screening – Is This Building a Good Fit?
---------------------------------------------------

1. Basic Profile
- Building type: 5–8 storey residential, ideally 10–20 apartments.
- Location: Chattogram or similar solar resource area.
- Monthly BPDB bill: Typically BDT 40,000–60,000 (target ~BDT 50,000 first).

2. Roof and Technical Fit
- Sufficient flat roof area for ~22 kW system (estimate 100–150 m² of usable space).
- Limited shading from adjacent buildings, water tanks, or trees during most of the day.
- Roof structure appears sound (concrete roof in good condition).

3. Decision-Making and Governance
- Clear decision-maker or active building committee.
- Willingness to share last 3–6 months of BPDB bills.
- Genuine interest in reducing electricity costs, not just casual curiosity.

If all three sections above are positive, proceed to detailed assessment.


B. Detailed Assessment – Before Preparing Proposal
--------------------------------------------------

1. Data Collection
- Obtain copies or photos of 3–6 recent BPDB bills.
- Record:
  - Average monthly bill amount.
  - Units consumed (kWh).
  - Tariff category and meter details.
- Conduct a site visit to:
  - Measure roof area and note obstacles.
  - Check for shading at different times of day (visual estimate).

2. Financial Modelling
- Input the building’s actual bill and consumption into the Netso financial model.
- Confirm whether a 22 kW system is appropriate or adjust system size.
- Generate key outputs:
  - Expected monthly solar generation.
  - Customer’s new bill with Netso.
  - Monthly savings for the customer.
  - Netso’s monthly profit during loan period and post-loan.

3. Commercial Fit Check
- Confirm that customer savings are meaningful (e.g., 10–20% bill reduction).
- Confirm that Netso’s margins and payback remain within internal targets.


C. Proposal Preparation
-----------------------

1. One-Page Proposal
- Use the standard “Netso One-Page Proposal Template”.
- Insert building-specific numbers for:
  - Current BPDB bill and kWh.
  - Proposed system size and Netso tariff.
  - Before vs After monthly cost and savings.

2. Attachments
- Short FAQ/objection-handling sheet.
- Optional: simple schematic or photo of rooftop with indicative array layout.

3. Internal Review
- Have another team member quickly review numbers and wording.
- Confirm that the minimum-generation commitment and contract term are clearly stated.


D. Meeting and Follow-Up
------------------------

1. First Presentation Meeting
- Walk the owner/committee through the one-page proposal.
- Focus on:
  - Today’s bill vs new bill.
  - Zero upfront, 7.00 BDT/kWh, 12-year term.
  - Minimum-generation commitment explained with simple analogy.
  - System ownership transfer after 12 years.

2. Handling Objections
- Use the Sales Playbook’s objection & answer section.
- Note down new questions to refine the playbook.

3. Next Steps
- If interest is positive: seek a non-binding Letter of Intent (LOI).
- If not ready: schedule a follow-up after sharing additional clarifications.
