# Netso

Netso is an energy infrastructure startup based in Bangladesh, transitioning from a distributed solar installer to a long-term asset-owning operator. It leverages the **[[Net Metering Guidelines 2025]]** and a unique **[[Solar Pergola]]** design to turn industrial and residential rooftops into income-generating micro-power plants. The company focuses on the **[[RESCO Model]]** (Build-Own-Operate) with a goal of reaching the **[[1 MW Threshold]]** for infrastructure-scale valuation.

## Key Facts

- **Founder**: [[Tazwar Mahtab]]
- **Stage**: Pilot Phase (3kW system commissioned, building 100kW pipeline)
- **Primary Product**: [[Solar Pergola]] (Standardized functional/aesthetic canopy)
- **Revenue Model**: Installation margin + 25-year PPA + asset management
- **Unfair Advantage**: Privileged access to **20-30 garment factory rooftops** via family industrial connections.
- **North Star Metric**: **[[Rooftop Acquisition Speed]]** (Goal: 1 MW within 18 months).
