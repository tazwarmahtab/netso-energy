# Source: Netso Complete Summary

Detailed executive summary of the Netso strategic implementation as of March 2026. This document marks the completion of Phase 1 (Documentation) and the transition to Phase 2 (Execution).

## Key Takeaways

1. **Policy Game-Changer**: Bangladesh's 2025 Net Metering Guidelines increased the solar capacity limit from **70% to 100%** of sanctioned load and enabled **single-phase** eligibility, expanding the addressable market by 200-250%.
2. **Standardized Product**: Netso focuses on the **Solar Pergola System**, a functional/aesthetic differentiation from commodity rooftop solar that turns roofs into income-generating micro-power plants.
3. **Multi-Tiered Revenue**: Business model includes installation margins, long-term PPA (Power Purchase Agreement) generation revenue, and asset management fees.
4. **Execution Bottleneck**: 100% strategic clarity but 0% operational execution as of late March 2026. Critical path: RESCO registration, first installation, and scaling to 1 MW.
5. **Market Opportunity**: $4B TAM with <0.1% penetration in a supply-constrained energy market.
