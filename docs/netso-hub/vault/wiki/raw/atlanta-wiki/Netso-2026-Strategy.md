# Netso 2026 Strategic Roadmap

This node synthesizes current market conditions and policy shifts in the Bangladesh solar sector into an actionable roadmap for [[Netso]].

## Strategic Core: The "Net Metering 2.0" Pivot

The 2025/2026 policy overhaul drastically expands the addressable market for rooftop solar.

### 1. Market Expansion (Commercial & Residential)
- **Single-Phase Eligibility**: Move beyond industrial 3-phase clients. Netso can now target smaller commercial and residential units.
- **100% Load Matching**: Design systems for max load efficiency; rooftop footprints can now be fully utilized for grid export.

### 2. Financial Model: Direct Cashflow
- **Direct Repayment**: Shift ROI calculations from "bill adjustment" to "revenue generation." Surplus is paid directly to bank/mobile banking accounts.
- **Tariff Competitive Pressure**: Tariffs at USD 0.04/kWh via competitive bids mean Netso must optimize its internal supply chain to maintain margin.

### 3. Immediate Action Items
- [ ] Integrate **Prepaid/Smart Meter** compatibility into the Netso monitoring stack.
- [ ] Develop a "Residential Prosumer" billing portal.
- [ ] Re-calculate utility-scale bid floors based on the USD 0.04/kWh benchmark.
