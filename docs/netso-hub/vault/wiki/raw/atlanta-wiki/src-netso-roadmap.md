# Source: Netso Master Roadmap

The ultimate 7-year strategic roadmap for Netso, outlining the transition from pilot phase to national energy infrastructure operator.

## Key Takeaways

1. **Strategic Imperative**: **Reach 1 MW as fast as humanly possible.** This is the threshold where valuation shifts from 1-2x revenue (installer) to 10-15x revenue (infrastructure).
2. **Phase 1: Foundation (0-3 Months)**: 50 kW installed, trademark filed, SOP manual v1.0, and 3kW pilot data documented.
3. **Phase 2: Traction (3-12 Months)**: 100 kW installed, first industrial project (100-500kW), seed round closed ($1M), team hired.
4. **Phase 3: Scale (Year 2-3)**: 1-5 MW installed, Series A, activation of garment factory pipeline, $5-10M debt facility (IDCOL).
5. **Phase 4: Platform (Year 3-7)**: 10-30 MW installed, growth equity, entry into Dhaka/Gazipur, VPP Launch, carbon revenue generation.
6. **Execution Focus**: If an activity doesn't accelerate **rooftop acquisition speed**, it is a lower priority.
