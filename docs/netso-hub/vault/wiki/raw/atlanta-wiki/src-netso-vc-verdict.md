# Source: Netso VC Verdict

Deep-dive assessment of Netso from a venture capital perspective, including the hardest investor questions and a "Summary Verdict" on investment readiness.

## Key Takeaways

1. **The Verdict**: Netso is currently "interesting but too early for institutional capital." It requires **1 MW installed capacity** and a documented rooftop pipeline to be venture-ready.
2. **Unfair Advantage**: The founder's **industrial access via family garment connections** is the primary hook for investors, as it provides a non-replicable channel for large-scale (500kW+) rooftop acquisition.
3. **Moat Analysis**: Netso's moat is built on:
    - Physical lock-in via 20-25 year rooftop contracts.
    - Financing track record (Idcol/Climate funds).
    - First-mover brand trust.
    - Proprietary solar performance data from Bangladesh.
4. **Unit Economics**: 
    - Residential (3kW): 7-9 year payback, ~90% margin post-payback.
    - Industrial (500kW): 8-10 year payback, 15-18% Equity IRR.
5. **Core Risks**: Hardware capital intensity, solo founder risk (need CTO/COO), and regulatory dependency on net metering policy.
