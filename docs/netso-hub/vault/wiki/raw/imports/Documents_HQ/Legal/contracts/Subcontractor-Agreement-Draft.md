## 2. Scope of Work

### 2.1 Subcontractor's Obligations

The Subcontractor shall:

(a) Perform all Work per the specifications in Schedule A
(b) Comply with all applicable laws, regulations, and standards
(c) Follow the 15-Point Rooftop Inspection Checklist
(d) Adhere to BNBC 2020 and Grid Code 2019 requirements
(e) Complete Work by the Completion Date
(f) Maintain site cleanliness and safety standards
(g) Provide all tools and equipment (unless specified otherwise)

### 2.2 Principal Contractor's Obligations

The Principal Contractor shall:

(a) Provide system design and technical specifications
(b) Supply equipment per Schedule B
(c) Conduct inspections and milestone sign-offs
(d) Make payments per Clause 4
(e) Provide site access during working hours

## 3. Safety and Liability

### 3.1 Section 43 Compliance (Bangladesh Labor Act 2006)

> **Critical:** The Subcontractor is the primary employer of all personnel deployed to the Site and bears full liability for workplace injuries, fatalities, or occupational illnesses under Section 43 of the Bangladesh Labor Act 2006 (Abetment of offences).

### 3.2 Subcontractor's Liability

The Subcontractor acknowledges and agrees:

(a) **Primary Employer Responsibility:** The Subcontractor is solely responsible for all employment obligations including wages, benefits, and working conditions
(b) **Worker Injury/Fatality:** The Subcontractor bears primary liability for any workplace injury, fatality, or occupational illness involving Subcontractor personnel
(c) **Indemnity:** The Subcontractor indemnifies and holds harmless the Principal Contractor from all claims, damages, and costs arising from Subcontractor's Work

### 3.3 Safety Requirements

The Subcontractor shall:

(a) Provide safety training to all workers before site mobilization
(b) Use personal protective equipment (PPE) at all times
(c) Complete the 15-Point Safety Checklist before each work session
(d) Report all incidents, near-misses, and hazards within 2 hours
(e) Comply with all site-specific safety protocols

### 3.4 Insurance Requirements

The Subcontractor shall maintain:

| Insurance Type | Minimum Coverage |
|---------------|-----------------|
| Workers' Compensation | As per Bangladesh Labor Act 2006 |
| Third-Party Liability | BDT 5,000,000 |
| Installation All-Risk | BDT 2,000,000 |

Certificates of insurance must be provided before mobilization.

## 5. Timeline and Delays

### 5.1 Completion Date

The Work shall be completed by [Date] unless extended per Clause 6.

### 5.2 Delay Penalties

- **Rate:** 0.5% of Contract Price per week
- **Maximum:** 10% of Contract Price
- **Grace Period:** 1 week

### 5.3 Extension of Time

Extensions granted only for:
- Force Majeure events
- Principal Contractor-caused delays
- Utility approval delays (beyond Subcontractor's control)

---

## 6. Force Majeure

### 6.1 Covered Events

- Natural disasters (cyclone, flood, earthquake)
- War, civil unrest, terrorism
- Government action preventing performance
- Utility grid unavailability
- Pandemic restrictions

### 6.2 Procedure

1. Notice within 48 hours
2. Mitigation efforts required
3. Timeline extension = duration of event
4. Termination right if 60+ days

---

## 7. Warranties

### 7.1 Workmanship Warranty

The Subcontractor warrants all Work for **5 years** from Completion Date:
- Watertight roof penetrations
- Proper electrical connections
- Secure mounting (wind load compliant)
- Grid Code 2019 compliance

### 7.2 Remedy Process

1. Principal Contractor notifies of defect
2. Subcontractor inspects within 7 days
3. Remedy completed within 30 days
4. Failure to remedy → Principal Contractor may engage third party at Subcontractor's cost

---

## 8. Termination

### 8.1 For Convenience

- 14 days written notice
- Payment for Work completed
- No termination fee

### 8.2 For Cause

Principal Contractor may terminate if:
- Material breach (7-day cure)
- Insolvency
- Force Majeure 60+ days
- Repeated safety violations
- Substandard work (2+ written warnings)
