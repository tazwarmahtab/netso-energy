## 1. Scope of Services

### 1.1 Included Services

The Service Provider shall provide:

(a) **Preventive Maintenance (2x/year):**
- Solar panel cleaning and inspection
- Inverter health check and firmware updates
- Cable and connector inspection
- Mounting structure inspection (torque check, corrosion check)
- Performance analysis report

(b) **Monitoring Services (24/7):**
- Remote performance monitoring
- Alert notification and diagnosis
- Generation reporting

(c) **Corrective Maintenance (as needed):**
- Troubleshooting and repair
- Replacement of faulty components (if covered under warranty)
- Emergency response within 48 hours

(d) **Reporting:**
- Monthly generation report
- Annual performance summary
- Incident reports (within 7 days of resolution)

### 1.2 Exclusions

The following are excluded unless expressly added:
- Component replacement outside warranty (labor charged separately)
- Damage from force majeure events
- Upgrades or modifications
- Cleaning more than 2x/year (additional cleanings at BDT 2,000/visit)

## 2. Service Fee

### 2.1 Annual Fee

| Description | Amount |
|------------|--------|
| Standard O&M Fee | BDT 5,000/system/year |
| Additional Cleaning (per visit) | BDT 2,000 |
| Emergency After-Hours Visit | BDT 3,000 |
| Component Replacement Labor | Actual + 15% |

### 2.2 Payment Terms

- Annual fee payable in advance
- Invoice issued 30 days before anniversary date
- Payment due within 15 days of invoice
- Late payment: 2% per month

---

## 3. Service Level Agreement (SLA)

### 3.1 Response Times

| Issue Type | Response Time | Resolution Time |
|-----------|--------------|-----------------|
| System Offline | 4 hours | 24 hours |
| Reduced Generation (>50%) | 24 hours | 48 hours |
| Reduced Generation (<50%) | 48 hours | 7 days |
| Routine Maintenance | 7 days | As scheduled |

### 3.2 Performance Guarantee

The Service Provider guarantees:
- **System Availability:** ≥ 95%
- **Performance Ratio:** ≥ 78% (normalized for weather)

If performance falls below guaranteed levels due to Service Provider negligence, Customer receives credit of BDT 500 per percentage point below guarantee.

---

## 4. Customer Responsibilities

The Customer shall:
- Provide safe access to the system (8AM-6PM)
- Report faults within 24 hours of discovery
- Allow Service Provider access for scheduled maintenance
- Not interfere with system operation
- Maintain property insurance on the system

---

## 5. Term and Termination

### 5.1 Initial Term

This Agreement is valid for **1 year** from the Effective Date.

### 5.2 Renewal

This Agreement automatically renews for successive 1-year periods unless terminated per Clause 5.3.

### 5.3 Termination

Either party may terminate:
- For convenience: 60 days written notice
- For cause: 30 days written notice (if material breach uncured)

### 5.4 Price Escalation

Annual fee escalates by CPI or 5% (whichever is lower) each renewal year.

---

## 6. Liability and Insurance

### 6.1 Liability Cap

Service Provider liability capped at 12x monthly fee (BDT 60,000) except for:
- Personal injury/death (unlimited)
- Fraud (unlimited)
- Gross negligence (unlimited)

### 6.2 Insurance

Service Provider maintains:
- Third-Party Liability: BDT 2,000,000
- Professional Indemnity: BDT 1,000,000
