## Key Legal Notes (from SHIELD / NETSO.ini Legal Agent)

> "Netso" as a word mark gives broad protection. A logo mark is narrower (only that design) but protects the visual identity. **File both** if Netso develops a distinctive logo — protects the brand on two layers. Consider filing the logo as a separate trademark once it is finalized.

> Class 37 is necessary for the installation/construction business. Class 40 is necessary for the electricity-selling/asset-owning business. Both must be filed together at the same time — adding a class later requires a new application and extra fees.
