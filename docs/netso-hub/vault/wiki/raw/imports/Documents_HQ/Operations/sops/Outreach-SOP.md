# Netso Factory Outreach Standard Operating Procedure

## Purpose

Govern how Netso acquires factory rooftop customers — from identifying prospects to signed OPEX agreements. This is the engine that drives the P0 metric: **1 MW installed as fast as possible, rooftop velocity over everything.**

**Owner**: Outreach-Coordinator (`/outreach` skill)
**Aligns with**: HORIZON (market intelligence), VOLT (NETSO.ini — sales strategy), Finance-Controller (OPEX math)

---

## Core Value Proposition — The Netso Pitch

Never lose sight of why a factory owner should say yes:

> *"Netso installs a solar pergola on your rooftop at zero upfront cost. You pay for the electricity you use at a rate lower than what you're paying DPDC right now. Netso handles everything — installation, maintenance, grid connection. You do nothing but sign a 25-year rooftop lease."*

**The two numbers that close deals**:
1. **Monthly savings**: (Current DPDC bill) − (Netso PPA payment) = net monthly savings in BDT
2. **Space value**: The canopy provides 500–2,000 sqm of usable shaded space (shade for workers, storage, outdoorbreak area) — this is a real operational benefit, not just electricity

## Step 1: Prospect Identification

**Owner**: Outreach-Coordinator + HORIZON (NETSO.ini research agent)
**Source**: Farzana Group connection is the primary moat — start there

### Target Factory Profile

| Characteristic | Target | Why |
|---------------|--------|-----|
| Location | Gazipur, Narayanganj, Chattogram EPZ | High electricity density, strong grid |
| Industry | Garments, textile, packaging, plastics | High kWh consumption |
| Monthly bill | ≥BDT 500,000 | Savings math works at this scale |
| Tariff | LT-C or LT-E | Higher tariff = stronger economics |
| Rooftop area | ≥500 sqm | Minimum for 50kW+ system |
| Ownership | Owner-operated (not rented) | Need clear rooftop ownership |
| ESG buyer | H&M, Zara, PVH, GAP, Gaborone compliance | ESG/statement value drives decision |
| Expansion plans | No planned construction on rooftop within 10 years | Long-term stability needed |

### Prospect Sources

1. **Farzana Group referral** (primary moat — use first, use hard)
2. **LinkedIn**: search "factory owner Gazipur" + "energy manager" + "facilities director"
3. **DPDC/BERC tariff data**: publicly available via BERC website
4. **Bangladesh Investment Development Authority (BIDA)**: factory registration data
5. **Horizon's weekly competitor/sector scan** (NETSO.ini): flag any new factory construction in target zones

## Step 2: Qualification

**Owner**: Outreach-Coordinator
**Timing**: Before any proposal is drafted

### Qualification Checklist (all must be YES or N/A justified)

1. **Does the factory own its rooftop?** (not leased, not shared ownership with another entity)
2. **Is the rooftop ≥500 sqm of shadow-free area?** (otherwise system size is too small)
3. **Is the decision maker identified?** (owner, CFO, or facilities director — not a middle manager without budget authority)
4. **Is the factory paying ≥BDT 8/kWh?** (confirm via tariff category — if too low, economics weaken)
5. **Are there any planned construction on the rooftop in the next 10 years?** (construction kills a 25-year PPA)
6. **Is the factory owned by a major international buyer?** (ESG driver is a multiplier — H&M/Zara buyers are gold)
7. **Is the factory in financial distress?** (avoid: solar PPA becomes a liability if the factory closes)

**If any item fails**: Mark CRM entry as DISQUALIFIED with reason. Focus energy elsewhere.

## Step 4: Discovery Call

**Owner**: Outreach-Coordinator (+ ATLAS if high-value deal)
**Duration**: 20–30 minutes
**Goal**: Answer the qualification checklist in real time, build the relationship, assess decision-maker authority

### Discovery Call Structure

1. **Warm-up** (2 min): Thank them for their time, confirm this is a good time
2. **Factory overview** (5 min): Understand their operations, production schedule, peak load hours
3. **Energy situation** (5 min): Current supplier (DPDC/DESCO/BREB), monthly bill, any issues with reliability
4. **Rooftop context** (5 min): Is the rooftop used? Shadow? Structural concerns? Any planned construction?
5. **Decision process** (5 min): Who else is involved in capital decisions? Board? Parent company?
6. **Netso model explanation** (5 min): How we work (no cost, we own the asset, they pay for electricity)
7. **Next step** (3 min): Site visit? Send proposal? Timeline?

### Post-Call Actions

- [ ] Update CRM: contact name, title, decision-maker status, monthly bill estimate, next action
- [ ] If qualified and rooftop confirmed: schedule site visit with Onsite-Commander
- [ ] If not qualified: mark DISQUALIFIED with reason, thank them and close gracefully

## Step 5: Site Visit & Feasibility

**Owner**: Onsite-Commander (technical) + Outreach-Coordinator (relationship)
**Deliverable**: Site feasibility memo (half page)

Onsite-Commander walks the site with the factory contact, conducts the 15-point structural inspection, and assesses suitability. Outreach-Coordinator stays close.

**Outcomes**:
- **Go**: Site enters Installation Tracker, move to proposal
- **Conditional Go**: Minor issues (e.g., some shadow, small roof section usable) — note conditions, still move to proposal with caveats
- **No-Go**: Structural issues, very small roof, shared ownership — thank them, close

## Step 6: OPEX Proposal Assembly & Delivery

**Owner**: Outreach-Coordinator + Finance-Controller + CANVAS (NETSO.ini content)
**Timeline**: Within 5 business days of site visit completion

See `Operations/templates/OPEX-Proposal-Template.md` for the full proposal structure.

### Proposal Assembly Workflow

1. **Finance-Controller** calculates site-specific OPEX math: monthly savings vs. Netso PPA charge
2. **Pilot-Analyst** provides proof data: pilot system performance photos and actual savings data
3. **Onsite-Commander** confirms site-specific design (kWp, panel count, canopy benefit description)
4. **Outreach-Coordinator** drafts personalized factory narrative (references specific factory data from discovery call)
5. **CANVAS** reviews for brand voice and polish
6. **ATLAS** reviews before delivery (for deals worth >BDT 1M estimated annual revenue)
7. **Delivery**: In person if possible (Outreach-Coordinator + ATLAS), else via email with read receipt

---

## Step 7: Negotiation

**Owner**: Outreach-Coordinator + ATLAS for material terms
**Rules**:
- Never negotiate on the fundamental model (zero upfront cost, Netso owns the asset)
- Can negotiate: PPA rate (savings split), canopy design premium, payment frequency, contract length
- Escalate to ATLAS if factory requests exclusivity clause, or requests changes to 25-year term
