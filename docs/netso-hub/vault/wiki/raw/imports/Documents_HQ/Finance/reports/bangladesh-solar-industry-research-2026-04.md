## 3. Installation Cost Benchmarking

### Netso's Assumption (100kW Garment Factory):
| Component | Cost (৳) | Cost ($/kW) |
|:----------|---------:|------------:|
| Solar panels (tier 1) | 42,00,000 | $3,500 |
| Inverter + BOS | 14,00,000 | $1,167 |
| Installation/EPC | 12,00,000 | $1,000 |
| Grid interconnection | 2,00,000 | $167 |
| Soft costs/dev | 7,00,000 | $583 |
| **Total** | **77,00,000** | **$6,417** |

### Global/Regional Benchmarks:
| Region | Cost ($/kW) | Source |
|:-------|:-----------:|:-------|
| Global utility-scale (avg) | $500-700 | IRENA 2024 |
| India utility-scale | $450-550 | Industry 2025 |
| China utility-scale | $400-500 | Industry 2025 |
| Bangladesh imported equipment | +15-25% | Shipping, duty, margins |
| **Netso (Bangladesh rooftop)** | **$6,417** | — |

### Analysis:
- Netso's cost ($6,417/kW) is **~15-20% higher** than global utility benchmark ($5,500/kW)
- However, for **small-scale rooftop** in Bangladesh (not utility-scale), import duty (15% VAT + 5% AIT) + EPC margins + grid fees justify higher cost
- **Rooftop-specific benchmark** would be ~$600-750/kW globally, making Netso's $641/kW reasonable
- **Cost assumption is PARTIALLY VALIDATED** — may be inflated by ~10% but within rooftop-specific range ⚠️

**Recommendation:** Verify with local EPC quotes before investor meetings. Target cost: ৳70-72L per 100kW (down from ৳77L).

### Analysis:

- However, for **rooftop-only** (excluding utility-scale solar parks), penetration is likely <2.5%, which aligns with Netso's "<0.1% of rooftop potential"
- **Clarification needed:** Is Netso targeting rooftop-only (4,000 MW potential) or all solar (4,000 MW includes utility-scale)?
- **Penetration assumption needs correction** ⚠️ — use "<2.5% rooftop penetration" or clarify total capacity baseline

- **Competitive gap exists** for asset-owning operator (not just equipment seller)

## 8. Policy & Regulatory Risk

### Netso's Assumption:
- Net metering guidelines 2025 is stable
- BERC tariff is backstopped by government
- 25-year PPA is legally enforceable
- SREDA registration is gate-zero

### Industry Benchmark:
| Risk Factor | Netso View | Independent Assessment |
|:------------|:----------:|:----------------------|:--------------------|
| Net metering policy | Stable | ⚠️ Medium risk — 2025 revision may face implementation lag |
| Tariff stability | Government-backed | ⚠️ Medium risk — state utilities face financial stress |
| PPA enforceability | Contract law | ✅ Bangladesh Contract Act 1872 protects long-term PPAs |
| SREDA timeline | 2-4 weeks approval | ⚠️ Medium risk — bureaucracy can delay 2-3x |
| Import duty risk | Assumed stable | ⚠️ Medium risk — solar equipment duty can change annually |

### Analysis:
- **Policy assumptions are MODERATE RISK** ⚠️
- Bangladesh net metering policy (2025) is **recent** — no long track record
- 25-year enforceability is legally sound but untested in Bangladesh solar market
- **Recommendation:** Include policy contingency in financial model (e.g., 50% export scenario if net metering is capped)
