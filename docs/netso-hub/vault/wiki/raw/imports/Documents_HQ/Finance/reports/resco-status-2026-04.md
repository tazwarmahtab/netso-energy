## Why This Matters (Ross's Brief)

RESCO registration is Gate Zero for Netso's business model. Without it:

| Blocker | Consequence |
|:--------|:-----------|
| Cannot sign RESCO/PPA contracts | Only EPC (one-time revenue, no recurring) |
| Cannot access IDCOL financing | 80% debt financing unavailable |
| Cannot participate in net metering | Export to grid at BERC tariff blocked |
| Investor credibility gap | No long-term contractual revenue |

**With RESCO registration:**
- 25-year PPA revenue contracts are legally enforceable
- IDCOL infrastructure debt available at ~9% p.a.
- Customer acquisition becomes easier (no capex for customer)
- Asset portfolio can be securitized or sold to infrastructure funds

**Bottom line:** This is a one-time regulatory hurdle. Once cleared, all downstream financing and revenue options open up. The draft is complete. The barrier is execution.
