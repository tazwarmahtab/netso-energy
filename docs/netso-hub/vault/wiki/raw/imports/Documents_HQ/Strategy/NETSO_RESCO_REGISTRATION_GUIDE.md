## ⚠️ IMPORTANT NOTES

1. **Free Registration:** RESCO registration is FREE. Do not pay anyone.

2. **No Obligation:** Registration doesn't obligate you to OPEX model. You can continue EPC operations.

3. **Annual Renewal:** Check if annual renewal required (typically yes)

4. **Compliance:** Must maintain compliance with SREDA guidelines

5. **Updates:** Inform SREDA of any changes (address, directors, contact info)
