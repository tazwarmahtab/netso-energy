## SECTION 4: BIPV, Thin-Film & Photovoltaic Glass Intelligence

### 4.1 Why This Research Matters

The concept images uploaded (solar canopy pergola, rooftop lounge with PV shade structure, people relaxing under glowing BIPV panels) reveal **intent beyond standard flat-roof rack installs**.

**Strategic Opportunity**: Dual-use rooftop solar
- Energy generation + lifestyle/usable space
- Major product differentiation for Netso
- Premium pricing (+20-40% revenue vs rack installs)

### 4.2 Thin-Film Solar — Critical Findings

#### Technology Comparison

| Technology | Efficiency | Key Characteristics | Netso Relevance |
|------------|-----------|---------------------|-----------------|
| **Amorphous Silicon (a-Si)** | 5.9-12.2% (lab) / 4-8% real | 40× higher light absorption, Staebler-Wronski degradation (15-35% loss first exposure), flexible | ❌ NOT for net metering |
| **CdTe (Cadmium Telluride)** | Up to 17.3% | Largest thin-film share, lower cost, **toxic cadmium** (import/disposal compliance) | ❌ Not SREDA approved |
| **CIGS** | Near c-Si | Expensive, toxic materials | ❌ Not SREDA approved |
| **Perovskite + Silicon Tandem** | >29% (lab) | Not commercially viable yet | ❌ Future tech only |
| **OPV (Organic PV)** | <11% | Affordable, short lifespan, BIPV aesthetics | ❌ Not for net metering |
| **PowerFilm a-Si (flexible)** | 0.2mm thick | 200 lux rated, indoor/low-light, IoT/military/camping | ❌ Wrong use case |

#### ⚠️ CRITICAL FINDING: Thin-Film & Net Metering

> **All 171 SREDA-approved modules are crystalline silicon** (mono-PERC, TOPCon, bifacial HJT).  
> **ZERO thin-film modules have SREDA approval.**

**Why Thin-Film Won't Work for Netso's Core Business:**
1. **No SREDA Approval**: None of the 171 approved modules are thin-film
2. **BSTI Certification Required**: Would need BDS IEC 61215 certification
3. **No Thin-Film Brand Certified**: Currently no thin-film brand has achieved this in Bangladesh
4. **Utility Rejection**: Net metering applications with thin-film will be rejected

**When Thin-Film IS Relevant:**
- ✅ Off-grid applications (no utility connection)
- ✅ BIPV aesthetics (non-generative decorative use)
- ✅ Canopies with battery storage (no grid export)
- ✅ IoT, military, camping (niche markets)

**Recommendation**: 
> **Do NOT pursue thin-film for Netso's net metering business.** Focus on SREDA-approved crystalline panels (Jinko 720Wp).

#### ⚠️ CRITICAL BIPV FINDING: SREDA Approval Status

> **BIPV glass (including Onyx Solar) is NOT on the SREDA approved module list.**  
> **BIPV is NOT eligible for net metering in Bangladesh under current regulations.**

**Why BIPV Won't Work for Net Metering (Yet):**
1. **No SREDA Category**: BIPV doesn't fit existing module certification framework
2. **No Approval Pathway**: SREDA has not created BIPV-specific standards
3. **Utility Rejection**: Net metering applications with BIPV will be rejected
4. **Different Product Class**: BIPV is building material first, power generator second

**When BIPV IS Relevant:**
- ✅ Off-grid luxury installations (no grid connection)
- ✅ Aesthetic facades (non-generative decorative use)
- ✅ Corporate sustainability showcases (branding over ROI)
- ✅ Future: When SREDA creates BIPV category

**Recommendation**: 
> **BIPV is a Phase 3 conversation** — only after SREDA creates a BIPV-specific approval pathway. Not actionable for net metering now.

### 5.2 Canopy Design Requirements (SREDA-Compliant)

| Requirement | Specification | Rationale |
|-------------|--------------|-----------|
| **Panel Type** | SREDA-approved crystalline (Jinko 720Wp) | Net metering eligible |

| **Spacing** | Per SREDA inter-row calculator | Avoid self-shading |
| **NOT BIPV** | Do NOT use photovoltaic glass | Not SREDA approved |
| **NOT Thin-Film** | Do NOT use a-Si/CdTe/CIGS | Not SREDA approved |

### 5.3 Canopy Product Line — Go/No-Go Decision
