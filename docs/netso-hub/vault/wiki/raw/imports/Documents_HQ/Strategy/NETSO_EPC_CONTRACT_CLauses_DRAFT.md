## 1. DEFINITIONS AND INTERPRETATIONS

### 1.1 Definitions

**"Agreement"** means this EPC Contract including all schedules, annexures, and appendices.

**"Commissioning Date"** means the date on which the System is fully installed, tested, and operational, and has received written approval from the Utility for grid connection.

**"Contract Price"** means the total fixed price payable to the Contractor as specified in Schedule A, inclusive of all taxes, duties, and charges unless otherwise stated.

**"Customer"** means [Customer Name], holder of electricity account number [Account Number] with [Utility Company].

**"Force Majeure"** has the meaning ascribed in Clause 6.

**"Grid Code"** means the Bangladesh Electricity Grid Code Regulations 2019 issued by BERC.

**"Net Metering Approval"** means written approval from the Utility for net metering connection as per SREDA Net Metering Guidelines 2025.

**"Performance Ratio"** means the ratio of actual energy output to theoretical energy output under standard test conditions, expressed as a percentage.

**"SREDA"** means Sustainable and Renewable Energy Development Authority, Bangladesh.

**"System"** means the complete solar photovoltaic system including panels, inverters, mounting structure, balance of system, and all associated equipment.

**"Utility"** means the local electricity distribution company (DESCO/DPDC/BREB/etc.).

## 2. SCOPE OF WORK

### 2.1 Contractor's Obligations (Turnkey)

The Contractor shall on a turnkey basis:

**(a) Engineering:**
- Conduct detailed site assessment using 15-Point Rooftop Inspection Checklist
- Prepare system design compliant with BNBC 2020 and Grid Code 2019
- Obtain all necessary technical approvals from Utility
- Ensure design optimizes energy yield (SREDA: 4.5 kWh/kWp/day Dhaka)

**(b) Procurement:**
- Supply all equipment per specifications in Schedule B
- Ensure modules on SREDA Approved List (171 modules)
- Provide certifications: BSTI, IEC 61215, IEC 61730 (modules); IEC 61727, IEC 62116 (inverters)
- Maintain chain of custody documentation

**(c) Construction:**
- Install per manufacturer specs and industry best practices
- Ensure 25-year minimum structural design life
- Implement safety protocols per Bangladesh Labor Act 2006
- Complete within Clause 5 timeline

**(d) Commissioning:**
- Performance testing and verification
- Obtain Net Metering Approval from Utility
- Customer training (Bengali + English O&M manual)

### 2.2 Customer's Obligations

- Provide accurate consumption data and sanctioned load info
- Obtain building owner/management permissions
- Ensure roof structurally sound (engineer cert if required)
- Provide site access 8AM-6PM
- Pay per Clause 3
- Cooperate with Utility for meter installation

### 2.3 Exclusions

- Structural roof reinforcement
- Waterproofing repairs (unless installation-related)
- Main panel upgrades beyond solar integration
- Tree trimming/obstacle removal
- Utility infrastructure upgrade costs

## 4. PERFORMANCE GUARANTEES

### 4.1 Guaranteed Performance

- **Annual Production:** ____ kWh/year (based on 78% PR, 4.5 kWh/kWp/day)
- **Performance Ratio:** ≥ 78%
- **System Availability:** ≥ 95% (excluding outages/maintenance)
- **Degradation:** ≤ 0.5%/year after Year 1

### 4.2 Performance Testing

- **Period:** 30 days from Commissioning
- **Method:** Compare actual vs. PVsyst simulation
- **Adjustment:** Weather/irradiance normalized

### 4.3 Liquidated Damages (Performance Shortfall)

**Formula:**
```
LD = (Guaranteed kWh - Actual kWh) × 10.30 BDT/kWh × Remaining Years (max 25)
```
**Cap:** 100% of Contract Price

## 5. TIMELINE AND COMPLETION

### 5.1 Standard Timeline (65 Days Total)

| Milestone | Day |
|-----------|-----|
| Agreement Signing | 0 |
| Advance Payment | 3 |
| Equipment Delivery | 25 |
| Installation Start | 28 |
| Mechanical Completion | 35 |
| Utility Approval | 52 |
| Net Metering Approval | 60 |
| **Commissioning** | **65** |

### 5.2 Delay Penalties

- **Rate:** 0.5% of Contract Price per week
- **Maximum:** 10% of Contract Price
- **Grace Period:** 2 weeks
- **Example:** 240,000 BDT × 0.5% × 2 weeks = 2,400 BDT

### 5.3 Critical: 8-Month Rule

⚠️ **SREDA 2025:** Installation must complete within 8 months of Utility approval or approval lapses. Contractor bears reapplication costs if delay is Contractor's fault.

## 6. FORCE MAJEURE

### 6.1 Covered Events

- Natural disasters (cyclone, flood, earthquake)
- War, civil unrest, terrorism
- Government action preventing performance
- Utility grid unavailability
- Pandemic restrictions
- Fire/accident (not caused by party)

### 6.2 Procedure

1. Notice within 7 days
2. Mitigation efforts required
3. Timeline extension = duration of event
4. Termination right if 90+ days

## 7. WARRANTIES

### 7.1 Equipment Warranties

| Equipment | Period | Coverage |
|-----------|--------|----------|
| Modules | 25 years performance, 12 years product | ≥80% output Year 25 |
| Inverters | 5-10 years | Parts & labor |
| Mounting | 10 years | Structural, corrosion |
| Monitoring | 2 years | Software/hardware |

### 7.2 Workmanship Warranty

**5 years** from Commissioning:
- Watertight penetrations
- Proper electrical connections
- Secure mounting (wind load compliant)
- Grid Code 2019 compliance

### 7.3 Claim Process

1. Customer notifies within 30 days of discovery
2. Contractor inspects within 7 days
3. Remedy within 30 days
4. Third-party remedy at Contractor cost if fails

**Critical Payment Condition:** Grid connection work only after written Utility approval (Electricity Act 2018 Section 7)
