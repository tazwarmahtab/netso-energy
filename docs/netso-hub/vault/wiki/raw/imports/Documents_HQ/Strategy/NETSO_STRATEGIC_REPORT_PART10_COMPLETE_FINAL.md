### Question 12: Why start residential?

**Answer**: Three strategic reasons:

1. **Brand Trust Building**: Residential customers talk to neighbors. 100 happy homes = neighborhood brand recognition. Industrial customers require more due diligence but follow credibility.

2. **Installation Process Validation**: Residential installs (2-3 days) allow rapid iteration on installation SOPs, safety protocols, customer communication. Mistakes are smaller scale.

3. **Generation Data**: Residential systems provide immediate generation data across different roof types, orientations, and weather conditions. Data improves industrial system design.

**Progression**: 
- Year 1: 80% residential, 20% industrial (learn, build brand)
- Year 2: 50% residential, 50% industrial (scale industrial)
- Year 3+: 20% residential, 80% industrial (industrial = scale engine)

### Question 13: How do you maintain systems for 25 years?

**Answer**: Four-layer maintenance strategy:

1. **Standardized SKUs**: All systems use same panel model, inverter model, mounting system. Reduces spare parts inventory, simplifies training.

2. **Remote Monitoring**: IoT-based monitoring detects inverter failures, panel underperformance, grid disconnection. 80% of issues detected before customer notices.

3. **Preventive Maintenance Schedule**:
   - **Quarterly**: Remote performance review, anomaly detection
   - **Annually**: On-site inspection, panel cleaning, electrical safety check
   - **Year 10-12**: Inverter replacement (planned capex)
   - **Year 20+**: Panel degradation assessment, replacement planning

4. **Maintenance Contracts**: 
   - Residential: ৳2,000/year (optional, 60% uptake expected)
   - Industrial: ৳50,000-100,000/year (included in PPA)
   - Revenue: ৳2-5M/year at 1,000 installations

**O&M Cost**: ~10% of revenue (industry standard for solar)

### Question 14: What is your biggest execution risk?

**Answer**: **Installation quality consistency at scale.**

**Risk**: As Netso scales from 10 to 100 to 1,000 installations, maintaining installation quality is critical. One major failure (roof leak, fire, injury) damages brand irreparably.

**Mitigation**:
- **SOPs**: Detailed installation manuals (checklists, photos, videos)
- **Training**: 2-week certification program for all installers
- **Quality Audits**: Random 10% inspection rate on all installations
- **Safety First**: Mandatory safety equipment, no shortcuts
- **Slow Scaling**: Cap growth at 20 installations/month until quality is proven

**Philosophy**: "Better to turn down 100 bad installations than execute 100 bad installations."
