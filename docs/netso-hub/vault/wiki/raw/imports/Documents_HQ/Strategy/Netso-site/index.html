This models SREDA net-metering as bill value using BERC retail tariffs (slabs / TOU). Conservative by design. Verify your category and latest tariffs via your utility bill / BERC updates.

TARIFF BASIS (SREDA)

Exports are netted against imports monthly at your consumer category's retail tariffs. There's no separate buyback rate. Excess typically carries forward as kWh credit.

DEMAND CHARGES

Demand charges (kVA) are not included in savings (they may not reduce 1:1 with solar). This calculator focuses on energy (kWh) value.
