## Part 1: Regulatory Compliance (SREDA Framework)

### Critical Compliance Facts

| Requirement | Status | Action Required |
|-------------|--------|-----------------|
| **SREDA Approval** | MANDATORY | Only crystalline silicon modules approved (171 panels) |
| **Thin-Film** | NOT APPROVED | Zero thin-film panels approved for net metering |
| **Inverter Certification** | Per-model required | Brand approval insufficient; each model needs separate certification |
| **Installation Deadline** | 8 months | HARD deadline from SREDA approval date |
| **RESCO Registration** | Required | Register as Renewable Energy Service Company |

### Approved Crystalline Silicon Modules (Partial List - 171 Total)

| Manufacturer | Model | Power Range | BSTI Reference |
|--------------|-------|-------------|----------------|

| Canadian Solar | HiHero | 600-700Wp | 351350 |
| JA Solar | DeepBlue 4.0 | 600-700Wp | 351355 |

**Key Fact**: SREDA approval is **mandatory**. BPDB will reject net metering applications without SREDA certification. Utility will not connect to grid without approved equipment.

### Grid Code 2019 Requirements

- **Inverter Compliance**: IEEE 1547, IEC 61727 (anti-islanding, voltage ride-through)
- **System Size Limits**: <10 kW (single-phase), >10 kW (three-phase)
- **Net Metering**: 1:1 credit for exported kWh, 20-year PPA
- **Capacity Cap**: Net metering capped at ~3% of peak demand (relaxing)
