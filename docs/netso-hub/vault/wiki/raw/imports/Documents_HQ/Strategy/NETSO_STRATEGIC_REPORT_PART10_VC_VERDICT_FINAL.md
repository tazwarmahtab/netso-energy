  - Month 4-6: Secure 1 factory LOI (500 kW - 1 MW)
  - Month 7-12: Complete 100 kW cumulative installed

---

#### Challenge 2: Hardware Business — Slower Growth, Capital Intensive
> **VC Concern**: "Solar is a hardware business — slower growth, capital intensive, not software margins."

**Counter-Argument**:
- **Reframe**: Netso is **not a hardware company** — it's an **asset-owning infrastructure company**
- **Comparable**: Real estate investment trusts (REITs) or utilities, not SaaS
- **Economics**: 
  - 8-10 year payback, then 15+ years of high-margin cash flow
  - 15-18% IRR on equity (attractive for infrastructure investors)
  - Debt financing (70-80% of capex) reduces equity intensity
- **Ask**: "Evaluate us as infrastructure, not tech. Use infrastructure valuation multiples (10-15x revenue), not software multiples."

---

#### Challenge 3: Regulatory Dependency
> **VC Concern**: "If net metering rules change, your economics break."

**Counter-Argument**:
- **Mitigation 1**: Shift focus to **industrial self-consumption PPAs**
  - Factories use 80-90% of generated solar internally
  - Export to grid is secondary (10-20% of generation)
  - Even without net metering, economics work with high self-consumption
- **Mitigation 2**: Policy trajectory is favorable
  - Government committed to 3,000 MW rooftop target
  - Net metering regulations updated as recently as 2023
  - Bangladesh imports 60%+ of energy — solar reduces import dependency
- **Ask**: "Underwrite the business with 50% self-consumption (no net metering). Upside from net metering is optionality."
