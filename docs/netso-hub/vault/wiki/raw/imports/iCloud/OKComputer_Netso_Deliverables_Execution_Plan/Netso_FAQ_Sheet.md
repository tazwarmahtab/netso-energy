# NETSO ENERGY - FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
## Factory Owner's Guide to Rooftop Solar PPA

---

## GENERAL QUESTIONS

### Q1: What is Netso's business model?

**A:** Netso operates under the Build-Own-Operate (BOO) model:
- We design, install, own, and operate the solar system on your rooftop
- You pay zero upfront costs
- You purchase the solar electricity from us at a rate lower than DESCO
- We handle all maintenance and operations for 20 years

---

### Q2: How much can my factory save?

**A:** Savings depend on your roof size and electricity consumption. Typical savings:

| System Size | Annual Savings (BDT) | 20-Year Savings (BDT) |
|-------------|----------------------|------------------------|
| 50 kW | 8-12 lakhs | 1.5-2.5 crores |
| 100 kW | 15-20 lakhs | 3-4 crores |
| 200 kW | 30-40 lakhs | 6-8 crores |

*Based on current DESCO rates and typical generation in Dhaka/Gazipur area.*

---

### Q3: What is the PPA term?

**A:** The Power Purchase Agreement (PPA) is for 20 years from the date of commissioning. During this period:
- You buy solar electricity at the agreed tariff
- Netso owns and maintains the system
- After 20 years, you have the option to buy the system at fair market value

## TECHNICAL QUESTIONS

### Q4: Will the solar system power my entire factory?

**A:** The solar system will offset a portion of your electricity consumption. The exact percentage depends on:
- Available roof space
- Your electricity consumption pattern
- System size installed

Typically, rooftop solar can offset 20-40% of a factory's daytime electricity needs. Any shortfall is automatically supplied by the grid.

---

### Q5: What happens when the sun isn't shining?

**A:** Your factory remains connected to the grid. When solar generation is insufficient (night, cloudy days), the grid automatically supplements your power needs. You won't experience any interruption.

---

### Q6: What about dust and pollution in Bangladesh?

**A:** Dust does affect performance, which is why regular cleaning is essential. Our O&M service includes:
- Monthly cleaning during dry season
- Bi-weekly cleaning during dust storm periods
- Performance monitoring to detect any issues

With proper maintenance, dust impact is minimized to 2-5%.

---

### Q7: Will the panels damage my roof?

**A:** No. Our installation process:
- Includes a structural assessment before installation
- Uses non-penetrating mounting systems where possible
- Ensures waterproof sealing at all penetration points
- Comes with a workmanship warranty

We also carry insurance to cover any unlikely damage.

---

### Q8: What if my roof needs repair in the future?

**A:** We design our systems with future access in mind:
- Panels can be temporarily removed if needed
- We coordinate with your maintenance schedule
- Minor repairs can often be done around the system

For major roof work, we can remove and reinstall the system at a pre-agreed cost.

## FINANCIAL QUESTIONS

### Q9: Is there really no upfront cost?

**A:** Correct. Netso bears all costs:
- System design and engineering
- Equipment procurement
- Installation and commissioning
- Insurance (first year)
- NEM registration

You only start paying when the system begins generating electricity.

---

### Q10: How is the solar tariff determined?

**A:** The solar tariff is set at a discount to your current DESCO rate:
- Typically 10-15% below current grid rate
- Fixed escalation of 2% per year (vs. 5% for grid)
- Locked for 20 years

Example:
- Current DESCO rate: BDT 12.50/kWh
- Netso solar rate: BDT 11.00/kWh (12% savings)

---

### Q11: How does billing work?

**A:** Monthly billing process:
1. DISCO net meter records import (from grid) and export (to grid)
2. Netso invoices you for solar electricity consumed
3. You pay Netso within 15 days
4. Any surplus export is credited by DISCO quarterly at BDT 6.45/kWh

You receive two bills: one from Netso (solar) and one from DESCO (grid supplement).

---

### Q12: What is the security deposit?

**A:** We require a security deposit equivalent to 2 months' estimated solar bill:
- Held as security for payment
- Refunded at the end of the PPA term (less any outstanding amounts)
- No interest is paid on the deposit

---

### Q13: What happens if I sell the factory?

**A:** The PPA can be transferred to the new owner:
- New owner assumes the agreement
- Terms remain the same
- We coordinate the transfer process

Alternatively, you can pay the early termination fee and we remove the system.

## REGULATORY QUESTIONS

### Q21: Do I need any approvals?

**A:** Netso handles all regulatory requirements:
- SREDA RESCO registration
- DESCO/DPDC Net Metering (NEM) registration
- Electrical inspectorate approval
- Building permit (if required)

You simply need to provide necessary documents and signatures.

---

### Q22: What is Net Metering (NEM)?

**A:** Net Metering allows you to:
- Use solar electricity on-site
- Export excess to the grid
- Import from grid when needed
- Get credited for surplus export quarterly

The net meter records both import and export, and you pay only for the net consumption.
