# NETSO FACTORY OUTREACH SCRIPT
## 2-Minute Phone Pitch for RMG Factory Owners/Managers

---

## PRE-CALL PREPARATION

### Research Checklist (30 seconds before call)
- [ ] Factory name and location confirmed
- [ ] Approximate roof size estimated (Google Maps)
- [ ] BGMEA membership status checked
- [ ] Export buyer information (if available)
- [ ] Current DISCO zone identified
- [ ] Sustainability certifications noted (LEED, EDGE, etc.)

### Materials Ready
- [ ] Netso company introduction (email ready to send)
- [ ] LOI template accessible
- [ ] Calendar open for scheduling
- [ ] Factory Savings Analysis template ready

---

## THE 2-MINUTE PITCH

### OPENING (15 seconds)

**Goal:** Establish credibility and get permission to continue

**Script:**
> "Good morning/afternoon, am I speaking with [Name], [Title] at [Factory Name]?"
>
> "Hello [Name], this is [Your Name] from Netso Energy. We're a Bangladeshi company helping RMG factories reduce electricity costs with zero upfront investment. Do you have 2 minutes?"

**If they say YES:** Proceed to Hook
**If they say NO:** "I understand. When would be a better time to call?" (Schedule callback)

---

### THE HOOK (30 seconds)

**Goal:** Identify pain point and create interest

**Script:**
> "[Name], I'm calling because I noticed [Factory Name] is in [Gazipur/Narayanganj]. Many factories in your area are paying BDT [12-15]/unit for DESCO electricity, and costs keep rising 5% every year."
>
> "We're offering something different: solar power at 10-15% below your current rate, with ZERO upfront cost. You only pay for the electricity you use."

**Key Points:**
- Mention their specific location (shows research)
- Quote realistic grid rates (builds credibility)
- Lead with savings percentage
- Emphasize "zero upfront cost"

---

### THE VALUE PROPOSITION (45 seconds)

**Goal:** Explain how Netso works and why it's different

**Script:**
> "Here's how it works: We install, own, and operate solar panels on your rooftop. You pay nothing for installation. You simply buy the solar electricity from us at a locked rate for 20 years—lower than DESCO, and it never increases as fast as grid prices."
>
> "We handle everything: design, installation, maintenance, even cleaning the panels. Your factory saves money from day one, and you have zero maintenance headache."
>
> "Plus, with export buyers asking for ESG compliance, this helps you meet their sustainability requirements."

**Key Points:**
- BOO model explained simply
- 20-year locked rate (hedge against inflation)
- Full O&M included
- ESG compliance angle (for export factories)

---

### THE PROOF (20 seconds)

**Goal:** Build trust with specifics

**Script:**
> "We're working with [X] factories in Gazipur already. A typical 100kW system saves BDT 15-20 lakhs per year. Over 20 years, that's 3-4 crore in savings."
>
> "We use Tier-1 panels with 25-year warranty, and we're registered with SREDA as a RESCO company."

**Key Points:**
- Mention similar factories (social proof)
- Quantify savings (specific numbers)
- Mention SREDA registration (credibility)

---

### THE ASK (10 seconds)

**Goal:** Get commitment for next step

**Script:**
> "[Name], I'd like to visit your factory for a free roof assessment. It takes 30 minutes, and I'll give you a detailed savings report. What day this week works for you?"

**Alternative Close (if hesitant):**
> "I can email you a one-page summary first. What's your email address?"

---

## OBJECTION HANDLING

### Objection 1: "We're not interested"

**Response:**
> "I understand. Can I ask—are you satisfied with your current electricity costs, or is the yearly price increase a concern? Most factories I speak with are feeling the pinch."

**Then:** Pivot to a smaller ask (email information)

---

### Objection 2: "We already looked at solar"

**Response:**
> "That's great—you're ahead of most factories. Did you look at buying the system upfront, or the pay-as-you-go model? Many factories find the CAPEX model too expensive. Our BOO model means you pay nothing upfront."

---

### Objection 3: "We don't have budget"

**Response:**
> "Perfect—neither do most factories. That's exactly why we designed this with zero upfront cost. You only pay for the electricity you use, at a rate lower than DESCO. It's savings from month one, not an expense."

---

### Objection 4: "Our roof is not suitable"

**Response:**
> "That's a common concern. The good news is we've installed on many different roof types—RCC flat, corrugated, even old roofs. Our site survey will tell us exactly what's possible. It's free and takes just 30 minutes."

---

### Objection 5: "We need to discuss with management"

**Response:**
> "Absolutely, I understand. Let me send you a one-page summary with the savings projection. You can share it with your team. What's the best email? And who else should I include in the conversation?"

---

### Objection 6: "We're renting the factory"

**Response:**
> "That's fine—we work with both owners and tenants. The agreement can be structured with the factory owner, or we can discuss a shorter term. Let me assess the site first, then we can explore options."

## TONE AND STYLE GUIDE

### DO ✅
- Speak with confidence and enthusiasm
- Use the factory owner's name frequently
- Listen more than you talk (60/40 rule)
- Ask open-ended questions
- Be respectful of their time
- Use Bangladeshi business etiquette
- Follow up promptly

### DON'T ❌
- Don't be pushy or aggressive
- Don't oversell or make unrealistic promises
- Don't badmouth competitors
- Don't use too much technical jargon
- Don't skip the research before calling
- Don't forget to follow up

---
