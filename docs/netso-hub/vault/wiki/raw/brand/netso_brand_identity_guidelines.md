# Netso Brand Identity Guidelines & Visual Asset Catalog

This document serves as the definitive reference for the **Netso** brand identity, detailing the "Heritage meets High-Tech" aesthetic and providing a comprehensive catalog of visual assets. It is designed to ensure consistency across all internal operations and external partnerships as Netso scales to become Bangladesh’s leading premium solar infrastructure provider.

---

## I. Brand Identity Guidelines

### 1. The Core Philosophy: Heritage meets High-Tech
Netso’s visual identity is a synthesis of Bangladesh’s rich artisanal history and the global vanguard of renewable technology. This aesthetic, termed **"The Digital Loom,"** bridges the gap between traditional craftsmanship and modern minimalism.

*   **Global Minimalism:** Clean lines, functional forms, and an "Apple-esque" focus on negative space.
*   **Local Heritage:** The integration of *Jamdani* geometric motifs into structural elements and digital interfaces, grounding the high-tech product in local culture.

### 2. Logo Specifications
The Netso logo is a minimalist mark designed for high legibility across physical structures (etched steel) and digital platforms.

*   **The Mark:** A stylized leaf/structure hybrid that incorporates circuit-like pathways within a organic silhouette.
*   **The Wordmark:** A custom, clean sans-serif with a geometric "o" that symbolizes a rising sun or a completed circuit.
*   **Usage Rules:**
    *   Maintain a minimum clear space equal to the height of the "n".
    *   Primary usage: Tech Silver on Dark Slate or Sustainability Green on White.
    *   Avoid shadows, gradients, or 3D effects on the primary logo.

### 3. Color Palette
The palette reflects a "Tech-Forward Sustainability" mindset. It balances the cold precision of engineering with the warmth of the Bangladeshi sun and landscape.

| Palette Segment | Color Name | Hex Code | Purpose |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Primary** | Tech Silver | `#A7A9AC` | Structural frames, high-tech hardware, UI backgrounds. |
| **Primary** | Sustainability Green | `#228B22` | Brand iconography, ESG reporting, sustainability accents. |
| **Secondary** | Solar Gold | `#D4AF37` | Energy flow indicators, premium membership accents, sun motifs. |
| **Heritage Accent** | Jamdani Blue | `#2E4172` | Traditional pattern overlays, deep contrast elements. |
| **Neutral** | Carbon Slate | `#2D2926` | Typography, structural shadows, premium finishes. |

### 4. Typography
To maintain a cinematic and premium feel, Netso utilizes a strictly hierarchical typographic system.

*   **Headlines:** *Montserrat Bold* or a similar high-fidelity Sans-Serif. Characterized by wide tracking and geometric precision.
*   **Body Copy:** *Inter* or *Helvetica Neue*. Focused on readability and clean, professional delivery of technical data.
*   **Principles:** Heavy use of white space, left-aligned text for commercial documents, and minimal use of italics.

### 5. Visual Principles: Cultural Fusion
The "Heritage meets High-Tech" blend is achieved through:
1.  **Pattern Integration:** Traditional Jamdani patterns are laser-etched into the Tech Silver aluminum frames of the pergolas.
2.  **Cinematic Lighting:** Marketing visuals use "Golden Hour" lighting (Dhaka sunsets) to highlight the warmth of the energy being captured.
3.  **Material Honesty:** Renders must showcase the juxtaposition of raw recyclable steel/aluminum against lush tropical greenery.

## III. Storyboard: "The Power of Presence"

The 30-second brand concept video is structured to move the viewer from the beauty of the heritage to the utility of the technology.

1.  **Scene 1 (0-5s): The Heritage Grain**
    *   *Visual:* Macro shot of a Jamdani weave transitioning into a laser-etched pattern on silver aluminum.
    *   *Audio:* Soft, traditional flute melody layered with a subtle electronic hum.
2.  **Scene 2 (5-15s): The Residential Sanctuary**
    *   *Visual:* Wide shot of the Dhaka Sunset Rooftop. A family sits under the pergola; lights softly glow as the sun hits the horizon.
    *   *Audio:* Ambient city sounds (distant) fading into a "clean energy" chime.
3.  **Scene 3 (15-25s): The Commercial Pulse**
    *   *Visual:* Transition to the Cafe Patio. A young professional plugs a laptop into the pergola post. The "Netso" logo on the post glows subtly.
    *   *Audio:* Upbeat, modern tempo. The sound of a connection being made.
4.  **Scene 4 (25-30s): The Netso Promise**
    *   *Visual:* The minimalist logo centers on a white background. Tagline: *"Heritage Meets High-Tech."*
    *   *Audio:* Logo sonic brand (two-tone melodic finish).

## IV. Strategic Considerations for Asset Use

*   **Internal Teams:** Use the Master Styleboard for all presentation decks and software UI development. Ensure all engineering diagrams use the "Tech Silver" and "Sustainability Green" color coding.
*   **External Partners (Architects):** Provide the 3D renders as "Aspiration Standards." All custom pergola designs must include the Jamdani-inspired etching patterns to maintain brand authenticity.
*   **Media/PR:** The video segments are optimized for social media (Instagram/LinkedIn). Ensure the cinematic "Golden Hour" tone is maintained in all photography.
