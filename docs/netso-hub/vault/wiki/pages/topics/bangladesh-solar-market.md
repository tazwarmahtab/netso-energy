# Bangladesh Solar Market

Bangladesh is an emerging rooftop solar market with an addressable ICP that overlaps well with Netso's RESCO model, driven by regulated grid tariff increases, mandatory rooftop solar rules, and growing electricity demand.

## Overview

Bangladesh has one of the fastest-growing electricity demand profiles in South Asia, with residential and commercial tariffs that have risen consistently over the past decade. The grid is unreliable — frequent load-shedding in most zones — making on-site solar generation attractive beyond just cost savings.

The SREDA-led net metering overhaul in 2025/2026 has unlocked the residential market by:
1. Opening single-phase eligibility (previously 3-phase only)
2. Allowing 100% load matching (previously 70% cap)
3. Mandating solar for new buildings >=1,000 sq ft rooftop

## Key Players

- **Netso** — Solo founder, pilot-validated RESCO operator, targeting 1MW
- **IDCOL** (Infrastructure Development Company Limited) — State-owned lender offering solar financing programs; potential debt facility for Netso scale-up (Year 2-3)
- **BPDB/DESCO/DPDC/NESCO** — Grid operators; bottleneck for interconnection approvals
- **Jinko Solar, Bluesun Solar** — Primary panel vendors
- **Emerging solar installers** — Fragmented market; few operate at Netso's sophistication level

## Current State (as of April 2026)

- Residential rooftop solar adoption is at "<2.5% penetration" (per finance model v4.0)
