# Factory Outreach Pattern

Netso's B2B sales motion for garment factory rooftop acquisition. The core insight: **the goal of every call is a 30-minute site visit, not a PPA.**

---

## The Cold Call Script (2-Minute Structure)

Source: `Netso_Factory_Outreach_Script.md`

### Opening (15 sec)
> "Good morning/afternoon, am I speaking with [Name], [Title] at [Factory Name]? Hello [Name], this is [Your Name] from Netso Energy. We're a Bangladeshi company helping RMG factories reduce electricity costs with zero upfront investment. Do you have 2 minutes?"

**If no:** "I understand. When would be a better time to call?" → schedule callback

### The Hook (30 sec)
> "[Name], I'm calling because I noticed [Factory Name] is in [Gazipur/Narayanganj]. Many factories in your area are paying BDT [12–15]/unit for DESCO electricity, and costs keep rising 5% every year. We're offering something different: solar power at 10–15% below your current rate, with ZERO upfront cost. You only pay for the electricity you use."

### Value Proposition (45 sec)
> "We install, own, and operate solar panels on your rooftop. You pay nothing upfront. You simply buy the solar electricity from us at a locked rate for 20 years — lower than DESCO, and it never increases as fast as grid prices. We handle everything: design, installation, maintenance, even cleaning the panels. Plus, with export buyers asking for ESG compliance, this helps you meet their sustainability requirements."

### The Proof (20 sec)
> "We're working with [X] factories in Gazipur already. A typical 100kW system saves BDT 15–20 lakhs per year. Over 20 years, that's 3–4 crore in savings. We use Tier-1 panels with 25-year warranty, and we're registered with SREDA as a RESCO company."

### The Ask (10 sec)
> "[Name], I'd like to visit your factory for a free roof assessment. It takes 30 minutes, and I'll give you a detailed savings report. What day this week works for you?"

## Objection Handling Matrix

| Objection | Response |
|-----------|----------|
| "We're not interested" | "I understand. Are you satisfied with current electricity costs? Most factories feel the pinch." → email one-pager |
| "We already looked at solar" | "Did you look at CAPEX or BOO/pay-as-you-go model? Many find CAPEX too expensive. Our model = zero upfront." |
| "We don't have budget" | "Neither do most factories — that's why we designed our model around zero upfront. Savings from month one." |
| "Our roof is not suitable" | "We've installed on RCC, corrugated, even old roofs. Site survey tells us exactly what's possible — it's free and 30 minutes." |
| "Need to discuss with management" | "Let me send a one-pager for your team. What's the best email? Who else should I include?" |
| "We're renting the factory" | "We work with both owners and tenants. The agreement can be structured with the factory owner. Let me assess first and then explore options." |

## Post-Call Actions

### If meeting booked
1. Send confirmation email within 1 hour (template provided in script)
2. Add to CRM with status: "Meeting Booked"
3. Prepare site survey checklist
4. Research factory background for meeting

### If email sent only
1. Send email immediately
2. CRM status: "Email Sent"
3. Schedule follow-up call in 3 days

### If no commitment
1. CRM status: "Not Interested" + reason noted
2. Follow-up in 6 months

## Building Qualification Checklist

Before proposing, the building must pass:

**A. Basic Profile**
- Building type: 5–8 storey residential (ideal 10–20 apartments) OR RMG factory
- Monthly BPDB bill: ৳40,000–60,000 (target ~৳50,000 first)
- Chattogram or equivalent solar resource area

**B. Technical Fit**
- Sufficient flat roof for ~22 kW system (100–150 m² usable)
- Limited shading (adjacent buildings, water tanks, trees)
- Sound roof structure (concrete, good condition)

**C. Decision-Making**
- Clear decision-maker or active building committee
- Willingness to share 3–6 months of BPDB bills
- Genuine interest, not casual curiosity
