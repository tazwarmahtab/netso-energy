# Netso FAQ — Factory Owner's Guide to Rooftop Solar

The canonical FAQ for B2B factory decision-makers evaluating a Netso rooftop solar PPA. Source: `Netso_FAQ_Sheet.md`.

## Business Model (Q1–Q3)

**Q: What is Netso's business model?**
A: Build-Own-Operate (BOO) / RESCO. Netso designs, installs, owns, and operates the solar system on your rooftop for 20 years. You pay zero upfront. You purchase solar electricity at a rate lower than DESCO. Netso handles all maintenance.

**Q: How much can my factory save?**
A: A 100kW system saves BDT 15–20 lakhs annually; BDT 3–4 crores over 20 years. (Based on current DESCO rates, Dhaka/Gazipur generation.)

**Q: What is the PPA term?**
A: 20 years from commissioning date. After 20 years, you can buy the system at fair market value or continue on a month-to-month O&M arrangement.

## Financial (Q9–Q13)

**Q: Is there really no upfront cost?**
A: Zero upfront. Netso bears all costs: design, equipment, installation, insurance (Year 1), NEM registration. You start paying when the system generates.

**Q: How is the solar tariff determined?**
A: Solar tariff set at a discount to current DESCO rate — typically 10–15% below grid. 2% annual escalation (vs. 5% for grid). Fixed for 20 years.

**Example:** If DESCO = ৳12.50/kWh → Netso solar rate = ৳11.00/kWh (12% savings)

**Q: How does billing work?**
A: Two bills:
1. **Netso invoice** — for solar electricity you used (pay within 15 days)
2. **DESCO bill** — for any grid supplement (you pay DESCO directly)
Surplus export (by DISCO) is credited quarterly at ৳6.45/kWh.

**Q: What is the security deposit?**
A: 2 months' estimated solar bill — refunded at end of PPA term (less any outstanding amounts). No interest.

## Technical (Q4–Q8)

**Q: Will the solar system power my entire factory?**
A: Typically offsets 20–40% of daytime electricity. The rest comes from the grid. You won't experience interruption — grid automatically supplements.

**Q: What about dust and pollution?**
A: Monthly cleaning during dry season, bi-weekly during dust storm periods. With proper O&M, dust impact stays at 2–5% performance reduction.

**Q: Will panels damage my roof?**
A: No. Non-penetrating mounting systems used where possible. Full waterproof sealing at all penetration points. Full workmanship warranty + insurance coverage.

## Legal & Contract (Q14–Q16)

**Q: Can I terminate early?**
A: After Year 5, with notice and early termination fee:
| Period | Notice | Early Termination Fee |
|--------|--------|-----------------------|
| Years 1–5 | Not permitted | N/A |
| Years 6–10 | 180 days | 50% of remaining projected revenue |
| Years 11–15 | 180 days | 30% of remaining projected revenue |
| Years 16–20 | 180 days | 15% of remaining projected revenue |

**Q: What if Netso goes out of business?**
A: PPA includes protections: system ownership transfers to you, O&M can transfer to another provider, equipment warranties remain with manufacturers.

## Regulatory & Timing (Q21–Q23)

**Q: How long does the process take?**
A: 3–4 months from first meeting to commissioning:
| Stage | Duration |
|-------|----------|
| Site assessment | 1–2 days |
| Proposal approval | 1–2 weeks |
| NEM registration | 4–6 weeks |
| Installation | 4–6 weeks |
| Commissioning | 1 week |

**Q: What is Net Metering?**
A: Net meter records both import (from grid) and export (from solar). You pay only for net consumption. Surplus export is credited quarterly.

**Q: Do I need any approvals?**
A: No approvals required from you. Netso handles SREDA RESCO registration, DESCO/DPDC NEM registration, electrical inspectorate approval, building permit.

## ESG & Certification (Q24–Q25)

**Q: How does this help with ESG compliance?**
A: Reduces carbon footprint ~1 tonne CO₂ per 10kW/year. Provides carbon offset certificates and sustainability reports for LEED/EDGE applications. Helps meet export buyer ESG requirements.
