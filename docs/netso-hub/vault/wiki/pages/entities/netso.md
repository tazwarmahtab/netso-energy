# Netso Energy

Netso Energy is a Bangladesh rooftop solar startup operating under the [[RESCO Model]] (Build-Own-Operate). Founded by [[Tazwar Mahtab]], the company is pivoting from a generic solar installer to a **Luxury Architectural Amenity** brand — positioning solar pergolas as premium outdoor infrastructure, not just electricity generation.

## The Pivot: "Architectural Amenity" vs. "Solar Installer"

Netso cannot compete as a commodity solar installer against low-cost generic competitors. **The strategic pivot** (confirmed via strategic intelligence, Q4 2025):

- **OLD:** Solar installer → competes on price per watt
- **NEW:** Luxury architectural amenity → competes on **value per square foot**

By positioning as "Solar as Furniture" / "Apple-inspired lifestyle technology":
1. Bypasses technical skepticism — sold as a design feature that "happens to pay for itself"
2. Unlocks top-20 Dhaka architectural firms as lead generation channels
3. Captures the "Urban Sanctuary" / "Social Hub" value that commodity solar cannot

> **Brand Motif:** The Jamdani geometric motif bridges Bangladeshi heritage and modern minimalism for the Dhaka elite market (Gulshan, Banani, Baridhara).

## Competitive Positioning

Netso avoids direct competition with:
- **Solar Electro, Super Star** — functional industrial utility installers (no aesthetic)
- **Commodity EPCs** — race to bottom on price per watt

Netso competes on:
- Aesthetic + functional dual-purpose product (pergola)
- RESCO asset model (long-term PPA revenue)
- Turnkey execution (design → NEM registration → commissioning)
- 25-year O&M included
