## Tax Framework (Bangladesh)

| Tax Item | Rate | Notes |
|---------|------|-------|
| Corporate tax (RE company) | 27.5% | Reduced from 35% under 7.5% renewable schedule |
| WHT on PPA payments | 7–10% | On electricity sale to grid/DISCOs |
| Capital gains (unlisted share) | 15% | NBR Section 31 |
| Import duty on solar panels | 0–10% | HS Code 8541.40.11 — verify current NBR circular |
| VAT on EPC services | 15% | Construction-related services |
| AIT on imports | 5% | Against corporate tax liability |
