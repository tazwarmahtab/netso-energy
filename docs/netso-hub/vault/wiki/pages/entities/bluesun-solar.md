# Bluesun Solar

Secondary solar panel supplier considered as an alternative or supplementary vendor to [[jinko-solar]].

## Overview

Bluesun Solar is a Chinese solar module manufacturer, positioned Tier-2 in the global market. Known for competitive pricing — a key consideration for Netso as it scales toward 1MW.

## Key Facts

- **Founded:** 2006
- **HQ:** Anhui Province, China
- **Market position:** Tier-2 solar module manufacturer; strong in price-sensitive South and Southeast Asian markets
- **Bangladesh presence:** Growing distributor presence; lower price point than Tier-1 alternatives

## Product Range (Bangladesh Market)

- Mono PERC panels: 400-540W range
- Competitive pricing structure designed for residential and commercial projects
- Generally sold through direct distributor relationships in Dhaka

## Pricing (Bangladesh, as of April 2026)

- Estimated 10-15% below Jinko Solar equivalent models
- 540W mono PERC: ~৳27,000-31,000 per panel (estimated, distributor pricing)
- Potential CAPEX savings: If used as primary vendor, could reduce system cost by 8-12% vs Jinko

## Strengths

- Lower price point — directly improves Netso's CAPEX and IRR
- Adequate performance for the Bangladesh market use case
- Growing Bangladesh distributor relationships

## Weaknesses

- Tier-2 classification — does not qualify for some project financing requirements
- Shorter warranty track record than Jinko
- Efficiency 1-2% below comparable Jinko PERC models
- Less Bangladesh-specific service infrastructure

## Use Case for Netso

**Bluesun vs. Jinko decision is a CAPEX vs. credibility tradeoff:**
- Jinko: Higher quality signal in customer conversations, acceptable for bank-financed projects
- Bluesun: Better unit economics, suitable for RESCO-model installations where Netso owns the system and absorbs performance risk

For the 0-100kW phase, Jinko is the primary choice. As Netso scales toward 1MW and beyond, Bluesun becomes relevant for margin optimization on larger projects.
