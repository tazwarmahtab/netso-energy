## Critical Fundraising Rules

1. Every metric in investor materials must have a live data source. Mark estimates as `(estimated)`.
2. TAM must use Bangladesh garment sector data with citation — not global figures.
3. Project the business the company CAN execute, not the business investors want.
4. YC application must be founder voice (Tazwar's voice) — co-write, don't write.
5. Cap table maintained in `models/cap-table.md` — updates within 24 hours of any equity event.
6. Investor updates = relationship tools, not performance apologies.
