# Jinko Solar

Primary solar panel and inverter supplier used by Netso. One of the world's largest solar module manufacturers.

## Primary Spec for Netso Installations

Netso's standard specification for all large installations (as per IDCOL Project Concept Note):

| Parameter | Specification |
|-----------|--------------|
| **Model** | Jinko Solar JKM720N-66HL5-BDV |
| **Type** | Bifacial Glass-on-Glass N-Type |
| **Power rating** | 720W per panel |
| **Warranty** | 25-year linear output warranty |
| **Quality tier** | Tier-1 (Bloomberg NEF) |
| **Certifications** | IEC 61215, IEC 61730 |
| **Why bifacial** | Captures reflected light; higher yield on white/reflective rooftops common in Bangladesh |

## Vendor Selection Rationale

1. **Tier-1 longevity** — essential for 25-year RESCO warranty backed by [[Netso]]
2. **Bifacial technology** — +10–20% yield vs. monofacial on reflective surfaces ( Dhaka rooftop, commercial buildings)
3. **25-year linear warranty** — matches [[RESCO Model]] 20–25yr PPA term exactly
4. **BNBC compliance** — cyclone-tested certification for Bangladesh monsoon/cyclone conditions

## Key Facts

- **Founded:** 2006
- **HQ:** Shanghai, China
- **Company size:** 50,000+ employees globally (as of 2025)
- **Market position:** Tier-1 solar module manufacturer; top 3 globally by module shipments
- **NASDAQ listing:** JKS
- **Bangladesh presence:** Widely distributed through regional distributors; service support available in Dhaka

## Product Range (Bangladesh Market)

- **Residential:** 400-550W mono PERC panels (suitable for Netso's 3-10kW residential systems)
- **Commercial:** 540-580W bifacial panels
- **Inverters:** Sungrow, Huawei, Fronius (sold separately or as package deals with Jinko panels)

## Pricing (Bangladesh, as of April 2026)

- Mono PERC 550W: ~৳32,000-36,000 per panel (distributor pricing, varies by volume)
- Typical 4kW residential system: 7-8 panels
- Supply terms: Letter of Credit or cash against delivery from Dhaka distributor

## Strengths

- Tier-1 certification (Bloomberg NEF) — qualifies for project financing
- Strong warranty: 25-year linear power output warranty, 12-year product warranty
- High efficiency panels (21-22% efficiency on current PERC range)
- Well-established distribution network in Bangladesh (multiple distributors in Dhaka)

## Weaknesses

- Price premium vs. Tier-2 Chinese panels — more expensive than alternatives like Bluesun
- Supply lead times depend on import logistics (port delays in Chittagong)
- After-sales service routed through distributors — response time varies
- Modules only — need separate inverter sourcing

## Recent Activity

- Expanded bifacial production capacity in 2025
- Strong pricing maintained through 2025 despite global solar module price corrections
- No major Bangladesh-specific product changes noted as of April 2026
