# Solar Pergola

Netso's flagship product: an **integrated solar pergola structure** that combines electricity generation with architectural shade, transforming rooftops into usable outdoor space. Positioned as a "Luxury Architectural Amenity" rather than a solar panel installation.

## Product Definition

A pre-engineered canopy structure with integrated bifacial PV modules providing:
1. **Shade + usable outdoor space** — turns hot, unusable rooftops into cool lounges/social hubs
2. **Electricity generation** — building-integrated PV with grid export via [[Net Metering Guidelines 2025]]
3. **Architectural enhancement** — certified by structural engineers for monsoon/cyclone loads

## Core Differentiators vs. Commodity Rooftop Solar

| Feature | Commodity Solar | Solar Pergola |
|---------|----------------|---------------|
| Primary value | kWh generation | Usable outdoor space + energy |
| Aesthetic impact | Diminishes property value | Enhances property value |
| Positioning | Utility product | Lifestyle/architectural amenity |
| Wind-load | Standard mounting | Cyclone-grade (250 km/h), monsooncertified |
| Roof penetration | Penetrating mounts | Non-penetrating where possible |
| IP potential | None | Patentable structural designs |

## SKU Strategy

Netso plans to standardize to **3–4 modular SKUs** to:
- Reduce custom engineering costs per installation
- Simplify inventory management
- Enable faster "plug-and-play" installation
- Lower CAC per kW

> Different SKU sizes for: residential (3kW), small commercial (10–20kW), RMG factory (50–200kW)

## Revenue Model

The pergola generates three revenue streams simultaneously:

1. **Installation margin** — markup on equipment + EPC (40% gross margin on BDT 600K avg)
2. **PPA generation revenue** — 20-year solar electricity sale at ৳7–9/kWh
3. **O&M fees** — bundled into PPA; separate ৳5,000/system/year in standalone OM contracts

## Manufacturing / Supply Chain

| Component | Source | Rationale |
|-----------|--------|-----------|
| Core tech (modules, inverters) | Imported (Tier-1) | 25-year warranty, BNBC/IEC certification |
| Structure | **Local fabrication** (BSRM/KSRM steel) | 22% cost reduction vs. imported; avoids import duties |
| Mounting hardware | Local | Cost + availability |
| Installer labor | Contracted (Netso Certified) | Scalable without full-time headcount |

## Risks

- Bangladesh monsoon/cyclone risk to structural integrity → MITIGATED: 250 km/h wind-load certification
- Local fabrication quality control → MITIGATED: strict QA with photo documentation per installation
- IP copying by competitors → PARTIAL RISK: design patents in progress
