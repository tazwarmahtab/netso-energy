# Netso 2026 Strategy

The strategic roadmap and positioning framework for [[Netso]] in 2026. Synthesized from the master strategy document, VC verdict assessment, and execution roadmap.

---

## The Central Strategic Thesis

**Reach 1 MW as fast as humanly possible.**

This is the single most important strategic imperative. At 1 MW, valuation shifts from 1–2x revenue (solar installer) to **10–15x revenue (infrastructure operator)**. Everything else is secondary to rooftop acquisition speed.

---

## The Positioning Pivot

**If marketed as a "Solar Installer"** → immediate commoditization, price wars with low-cost generic competitors.

**The Pivot:** Netso operates in the **Luxury Architectural Amenity** category. Solar is "a design feature that happens to pay for itself."

This changes the entire GTM:
- **OLD metric:** Price per watt
- **NEW metric:** Value per square foot
- **Sales channel:** Top 20 Dhaka architectural firms → lead generation (not traditional solar contractors)
- **Social value:** "Urban Sanctuary" / "Social Hub" — increasing café revenue or apartment resale premium

## Phase-by-Phase Execution (7-Year Roadmap)

### Phase 1: Foundation (0–3 Months)
- **Target:** 50 kW installed
- Finalize 3–4 modular pergola SKUs
- Complete 3 pilot installations
- Secure supply agreements (Jinko, fabricators)
- Execute SREDA RESCO registration
- Finalize local fabrication agreement or MOUs
- Launch website with 3D renders

### Phase 2: Traction (3–12 Months)
- **Target:** 100 kW installed
- Launch marketing campaigns + demonstration sites
- Sign distribution/install agreements with architects, real estate, hospitality chains
- Offer CAPEX and OPEX financing with clear ROI calculations
- Implement remote monitoring + maintenance service
- **$1M seed round closed**
- Hire: Solar engineer, B2B sales, monitoring ops

### Phase 3: Scale (Year 2–3)
- **Target:** 1–5 MW installed
- Activate garment factory pipeline (20–30 rooftops via family network)
- Series A raise
- $5–10M IDCOL debt facility activated
- Entry into Dhaka/Gazipur commercial market
- Train installation teams, enforce QA at scale

### Phase 4: Platform (Year 3–7)
- **Target:** 10–30 MW installed
- Growth equity round
- Dhaka + Gazipur dominant + expand to Narayanganj/Damurhh/Cumilla
- VPP Launch (Virtual Power Plant) — aggregate rooftops
- Carbon credit revenue generation
- Explore E-mobility / EV charging integration

## Regulatory Anchor

| Policy | Impact on Netso |
|--------|----------------|
| **Net Metering Guidelines 2025** | 100% sanctioned load cap, single-phase eligible, direct bank payout for surplus. Addressable market expanded 200–250%. |
| **National Rooftop Solar Programme** | Government mandate for solar on all public buildings. Private companies install and maintain for rent. |
| **Renewable Energy Policy 2025** | 20% electricity from renewables by 2030, 30% by 2040. |
| **July 2025 Grid Connection Rule** | All new grid connections must include 10–15% rooftop PV. Non-negotiable demand floor. |
