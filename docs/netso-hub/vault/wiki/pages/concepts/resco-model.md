# RESCO Model

Netso operates under the **RESCO (Renewable Energy Service Company) model** — the dominant rooftop solar business model in Bangladesh under SREDA's Net Metering Framework 2.0.

## Thesis

Netso owns the solar system, installs it at zero upfront cost to the customer, generates and sells electricity to the customer at a tariff below the grid rate, and retains ownership of the system for the duration of a long-term PPA (typically 20-25 years).

## How It Works

**Customer pays nothing upfront.** Netso finances, owns, and maintains the system.

The customer signs a 20-25 year Power Purchase Agreement (PPA) with Netso. The customer pays Netso for electricity generated by the solar system at a contracted rate — typically ৳7-9/kWh — which is below the grid retail tariff of ~৳10-12/kWh. The customer saves on their electricity bill. Netso earns revenue from the electricity sale.

Netso can also sell surplus electricity back to the grid under Net Metering 2.0, generating additional revenue. As of the 2025 guidelines, surplus is no longer just bill credit — it can be paid directly to the customer's bank account or mobile wallet (bkash/Nagad).

**System sizing:** Under Net Metering 2025, systems can be sized at up to 100% of the customer's sanctioned load (previously capped at 70%). Prepaid and smart meter compatibility is now mandated.

**Grid interconnection:** Requires BPDB/NTDCL approval process — DESCO/DPDC/NESCO zones each have their own process.

## Evidence For

- [[pilot-unit-economics]] pilot validated IRR of 42% and payback of 2.6 years — strong proof the model works at 4kW residential scale
- [[net-metering-2025]] now allows direct bank payout for surplus, improving cash flow for Netso
- [[net-metering-2025]] allows 100% load matching, enabling larger system designs and higher IRR
- Mandatory rooftop solar for new buildings >=1,000 sq ft creates a growing pipeline of mandated customers
- [[bangladesh-solar-market]] Bangladesh electricity tariffs have consistently risen, making solar savings more compelling over time

## Evidence Against

- [[trust-blocker-pattern]] Customer distrust of solar installers is the primary conversion blocker — zero upfront can feel "too good to be true"
- Grid interconnection approval from BPDB/DESCO can take 3-6 months, delaying revenue commencement
- No long-term pilot data beyond 1-2 years on system degradation and performance in Bangladesh climate
- Households with bills <৳20k/mo do not generate sufficient IRR to make RESCO viable for them

## How Netso Uses It

- [[bangladesh-solar-market]] ICP is households and businesses paying ৳40-80k/month — enough bill headroom for strong savings and Netso IRR >25%
- [[trust-blocker-pattern]] Sales pitch leads with savings, not technology — trust must be established before financial case
- PPA term: 25 years standard (O&M included at ৳5,000/system/year)
- Netso maintains system, handles all regulatory compliance — customer hands-off after signing
- [[pilot-unit-economics]] 4kW pilot system is the reference sale — used in trust-building and ROI proof
