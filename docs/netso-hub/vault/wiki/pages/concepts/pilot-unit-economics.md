# Pilot Unit Economics

Netso's validated 4.3kW residential pilot system demonstrates the financial viability of the RESCO model. As of May 2026, these are the only real-world numbers in the company.

## Thesis

A 4.3kW residential system at ৳2.5L total system cost (conservative, actual ৳2.2L) generates IRR of 42% and payback of 2.6 years — confirming the RESCO model's viability within the target ICP.

## How Netso Uses This

**In sales conversations:**
- The 4kW pilot is the reference case for every customer conversation
- "We have a 4kW system installed in [area] — you're welcome to see it" is the primary trust-building move
- ROI projections are shown using the pilot as the demonstration unit

**In investor conversations:**
- 42% IRR is the headline number for equity/storytelling
- 2.6yr payback (customer RESCO experience) is faster than most solar markets globally
