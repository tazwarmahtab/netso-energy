## Key Takeaways

- The strongest repeated strategic framing is not generic EPC and not residential pergola-first. It is a Bangladesh tech-enabled RESCO/OPEX infrastructure company for factory rooftops, especially RMG/garment factories.
- The pergola/canopy product remains valuable as a premium differentiator and visual proof asset, but captured conversations repeatedly warn that it should not distract from the commercial/factory RESCO wedge.
- Farzana Group / family garment network access is treated as Netso's most important early moat because it reduces customer acquisition friction and creates warm access to factory rooftops.
- The core execution unlock is first signed/warm factory customer, roof walk, proforma supplier quote, IDCOL checklist, and enforceable OPEX/PPA legal structure.
- Captured financial reasoning says wealth creation comes from owned solar assets after debt repayment, not from installer margin.
- AI conversations consistently warn that planning, website iteration, and generic modeling are secondary until customer proof and verified financing terms exist.
- Platform work should support sales proof and context persistence, not become a premature operations system.

## Extracted Business Facts From AI Conversations

- Netso is repeatedly described as a rooftop solar RESCO/OPEX model where Netso finances, owns, installs, operates, and sells electricity/savings to customers.
- Primary target in later strategic conversations is garment/RMG factories, not small residential homes.
- Factory sites are favored because daytime load matches solar generation and a single factory can equal many residential customers in revenue.
- Residential small-system economics are repeatedly challenged; a 20-unit / BDT 50k monthly bill case is described as non-viable, while larger commercial bills or high-load buildings are more plausible.
- The business model must model both customer OPEX/PPA payments and NEM surplus revenue.
- `65% of old bill` appears as a model-derived charge level that may preserve customer savings while covering debt, but it has not been market-tested.
- Deal 1 / first live customer is treated as the activation event for IDCOL credibility, investor credibility, and later scale.

## Extracted Financing / Regulatory Context

- Existing wiki and captured conversations conflict on IDCOL terms. Existing pages cite `80% debt @ 9%, 15 years`; captured AI conversations cite `80% debt @ 6%, 10 years, 1-year grace, quarterly repayments`.
- Captured conversations warn that quarterly repayment timing creates a cash-flow mismatch versus monthly customer collections.
- BRAC Bank project finance is framed as likely premature before signed customers, but BRAC/SME/corporate channels and Bangladesh Bank green/startup programs may matter later.
- EBL Startup Explorer and Bangladesh Bank startup financing were discussed, but captured claims require official verification.
- Tax holiday eligibility for rooftop RESCO projects is unresolved. Captured conversations mention renewable energy tax incentives but warn rooftop eligibility may differ from utility-scale BOO.
- SREDA/RESCO status is inconsistent across conversations and must be verified by certificate/status evidence.

## Extracted Product / Platform Context

- Durable Netso AI architecture should be file-based and model-agnostic: markdown source of truth, raw-source preservation, embeddings/RAG, and a living system prompt.
- Azure AI Foundry agent work is useful only if it builds on the Netso knowledge base rather than replacing it with another silo.
- MVP platform architecture from captured conversations: public website (`Next.js`), mobile app (`React Native`), shared backend (`Supabase`), integration layer (`inverter data + Make.com automation`).
- Supabase Auth with RLS is required for shared customer/internal/investor data.
- Mobile MVP scope: lead capture, pilot monitoring as live sales proof, field sales enablement, proposal presentation, rooftop photo capture, and signature capture.
- Operations management is excluded from MVP until Netso has enough live sites to justify it.

## Extracted Website / Brand Context

- Website direction: cinematic luxury-industrial, premium infrastructure, near-black and amber palette, strong typography, film grain, scroll storytelling, and realistic Dhaka rooftop imagery.
- Website should explain zero upfront, savings, NEM, technical credibility, O&M, warranties, and proof.
- The customer calculator assumptions captured from AI conversations are useful for prototyping but need official tariff and generation verification.
- ChatGPT visual prompts emphasize realistic local context: dense Dhaka rooftops, concrete, water tanks, satellite dishes, humidity, and non-Western architecture.
- Logo and brand advice warns against generic solar-panel icons; the brand should feel like infrastructure and premium energy.

## Extracted Funding / Application Context

- YC framing should lead with `Bangladesh RESCO: own solar on factory rooftops` or similar, emphasizing factory rooftops, zero-upfront OPEX, and deployment pipeline.
- a16z Speedrun appears relevant for capital, credits, global-founder support, visa/banking help, and Bangladesh eligibility, but application strength improves after Deal 1 or a credible LOI.
- G-Force and earlier grant framings use more lifestyle/pergola language; this may be grant-useful but conflicts with the later factory RESCO consensus.
- Arch Grants analysis exists, but the captured 2026 deadline was March 31, making it historical unless a future cycle is reopened.
- Current funding stage is best described as pre-seed; LOIs help, but signed contracts and real deployments matter much more.

## Critical Contradictions

- Pilot size/status: `3kW`, `3.5kW`, and `4kW` all appear across sources.
- IDCOL terms: `9% / 15-year` versus `6% / 10-year / quarterly / 1-year grace`.
- RESCO/SREDA status: conversations alternate between registered/approved, partnership/framework, and in-progress.
- Customer segment: residential premium pergola versus commercial/RMG RESCO.
- CAPEX: basic residential rooftop numbers versus premium commercial pergola numbers.
- Traction: pilot live, equipment purchased, first customer unsigned, and application-ready traction claims appear in different places.
- Market claims such as tariff levels, 5GW rooftop potential, Solaric pipeline, tax exemptions, and Bangladesh Bank financing need official verification.
