## 1. Battery Energy Storage System (BESS) Performance Guarantee

### Clause 1.1: System Capacity & Efficiency Thresholds
1. **Tier-1 Chemistry:** The Battery Energy Storage System (BESS) shall utilize Tier-1 Lithium Iron Phosphate (LiFePO4 / LFP) cell chemistry with integrated Battery Management System (BMS).
2. **Round-Trip Efficiency (RTE):** Netso guarantees a minimum AC-to-AC Round-Trip Efficiency of **$\ge 80.0\%$** across Year 1 through Year 10 of commercial operation.
3. **Capacity Retention Floor:** Netso guarantees that the BESS shall retain at least **$70.0\%$ of initial nameplate usable capacity** (at 90% Depth of Discharge) at the conclusion of Year 10.

### Clause 1.2: Degradation & Mid-Life Overhaul
1. **Annual Degradation:** Linear degradation allowance of $\le 2.0\%\text{ p.a.}$ is factored into the operational model.
2. **Year-11 Cell Module Refresh:** Netso maintains an irrevocable Capital Replacement Reserve (budgeted at BDT 10,000/kWh of storage capacity) dedicated to BESS cell module replacement and inverter capacitor overhauls during Year 11 of the 20-year term.
3. **Uptime & Outage Response:** Netso guarantees $\ge 98.0\%$ hardware availability. In the event of a BESS fault, Netso shall deploy a technician on-site within twenty-four (24) business hours.

## 2. Residential Multi-Story Building: Property Deed Attachment & HOA Covenant

### Clause 2.1: Irrevocable Rooftop Lease & Easement
1. **Right of Access & Solar Easement:** The Building Management Committee / Flat Owners' Association (hereinafter the "HOA") grants Netso Energy an exclusive, irrevocable lease and solar access easement over the designated rooftop footprint for twenty (20) years.
2. **Deed Attachment (Covenant Running with the Land):**
   > *"This Power Purchase Agreement and the underlying Rooftop Solar Lease constitute a binding restrictive covenant and servitude attached directly to the property's Title Deed and Mutation Records under the Registration Act 1908. Any subsequent purchaser, transferee, or heir of an apartment unit or rooftop fractional share takes title subject to the terms of this Agreement."*
3. **HOA Committee Continuity:** In the event of annual or periodic HOA committee turnover, the newly elected President, General Secretary, and Treasurer automatically assume full legal obligations of the predecessor committee under this Agreement without requiring novation.

### Clause 2.2: Payment & Dispute Mechanics
1. **Single Counterparty Billing:** Netso bills the HOA as a single corporate entity for all solar power delivered to the building's common-service distribution panel. The HOA collects solar charges as an integrated line item within the monthly building maintenance surcharge.
2. **Disconnection Remedy:** In the event of an uncured payment default exceeding sixty (60) days, Netso reserves the right to isolate solar power feed into the common service panel and operate the system in 100% Grid-Export mode to monetize generation directly via SREDA Net-Metering credits until arrears are cleared.

## 3. Professional Indemnity, Insurance & Structural Liability

### Clause 3.1: Structural Roof Integrity & Certification
1. **Condition Precedent (CDA / RAJUK Sign-Off):** Prior to equipment mobilization, Netso shall procure an independent structural engineering assessment conducted by a BUET-certified or RAJUK/CDA-enlisted structural engineer confirming the roof slab possesses adequate dead and live load capacity for the elevated Solar Pergola.
2. **Non-Penetrative Waterproofing Warranty:** The Solar Pergola mounting structure shall utilize non-invasive ballast framing or pedestals anchored directly into reinforced concrete column heads. Netso warrants that no roof membrane waterproofing shall be compromised and provides a ten (10) year leak-free guarantee over the structural footprint.

### Clause 3.2: Comprehensive Insurance Coverage
Netso shall maintain the following insurance policies throughout the 20-year PPA term:
1. **Erection All Risks (EAR) & Transit Insurance:** Covers 100% of equipment value during mobilization and installation.
2. **Third-Party Commercial General Liability (CGL):** Minimum coverage of **BDT 50,000,000 (Tk 5.00 Crore)** per occurrence against property damage or bodily injury.
3. **Force Majeure & Cyclone / Monsoon Windstorm Insurance:** Structure rated to withstand **210 km/h wind gusts** (BNBC Category-4 Cyclone Rating). Physical damage resulting from named storms shall be repaired under Netso’s comprehensive property insurance at zero cost to the building owner.
